UNIT I Semiconductor Theory & Diodes

1. Energy Band Theory of Crystals

Isolated atom mein electrons ke discrete energy levels hote hain. Jab bahut saare atoms paas aakar crystal banate hain, to neighbouring atoms ke levels overlap karke energy bands ban jaate hain (Pauli exclusion principle ki wajah se ek level split ho jaata hai).

  • Valence Band (VB): outermost (valence) electrons wala band; 0 K par completely filled.
  • Conduction Band (CB): free electrons ka band; isme aaye electrons current carry karte hain.
  • Forbidden Energy Gap (Eg): VB top aur CB bottom ke beech ka gap, jahan koi electron allowed nahi. Unit: eV (1 eV = 1.6×10−19 J).

3. Intrinsic & Extrinsic Semiconductors

Intrinsic (pure) semiconductor

  • Pure Si/Ge; room temp par kuch covalent bonds tootte hain → electron-hole pairs.
  • n = p = ni (intrinsic concentration). Si: ni ≈ 1.5×1010/cm³ at 300 K.
  • Fermi level EF lagbhag band gap ke beech mein.

Extrinsic (doped) semiconductor

  • N-type: pentavalent impurity (P, As, Sb) = donor. Majority carriers = electrons. EF CB ke paas.
  • P-type: trivalent impurity (B, Al, Ga, In) = acceptor. Majority = holes. EF VB ke paas.
Formula Mass action law: n·p = ni2  |  Conductivity: σ = q(nμn + pμp)  |  N-type: n ≈ ND, p = ni2/ND  |  Intrinsic: σi = q ni(μn+μp)

4. Theory of P-N Junction

P aur N type ko ek hi crystal mein milane par junction banta hai. Concentration gradient ki wajah se holes P→N aur electrons N→P diffuse karte hain aur recombine ho jaate hain. Junction ke paas sirf immobile ions bachte hain (P side negative, N side positive) → depletion region (space-charge region). Ye ions ek electric field banate hain jo aur diffusion ko rokta hai → barrier potential V0 (Si ≈ 0.7 V, Ge ≈ 0.3 V).

FormulaV0 = VT lnNANDni2,   VT = kTq = T11600 ≈ 26 mV at 300 K
  • Forward bias (P → +ve): barrier kam hota hai, depletion width ghatti hai, large majority-carrier current (mA).
  • Reverse bias (P → −ve): barrier badhta hai, depletion width badhti hai, sirf tiny minority-carrier current I0 (nA–μA) = reverse saturation current.

5. Ideal & Practical Diode; Characteristics & Operation

  • Ideal diode: forward mein short circuit (V=0, R=0), reverse mein open circuit (I=0). Perfect switch.
  • Practical diode: conduction tab hota hai jab V > cut-in / knee voltage Vγ (Si 0.7 V, Ge 0.3 V); forward resistance rf finite hota hai; reverse mein small I0 flow karta hai; VBR par breakdown.
  • Static resistance R = VD/ID; dynamic resistance rd = ΔV/ΔI = ηVT/ID (≈ 26 mV/ID).
Diode (Shockley) EquationI = I0 ( eV/ηVT − 1 )η = ideality factor (1 for Ge, ≈2 for Si at low current). Forward large V: I ≈ I0eV/ηVT; reverse: I ≈ −I0.

6. Temperature & Breakdown Characteristics

Temperature effect

  • I0 har 10 °C rise par double: I0(T2) = I0(T1)·2(T2−T1)/10.
  • Same current ke liye VD ≈ −2.5 mV/°C (Si) ghatta hai → forward curve left shift.
  • Breakdown voltage temperature ke saath change hota hai (neeche dekho).

7. Load Line Analysis

Circuit: source VS, series resistor RL, diode. KVL: VS = IDRL + VD. Ye ek straight line hai = DC load line.

  • ID = 0 → VD = VS (x-intercept)
  • VD = 0 → ID = VS/RL (y-intercept)
  • Load line aur diode curve ka intersection = Q-point (operating point: IDQ, VDQ).

9. Piecewise Linear DC Model of Diode

Exponential curve ko straight-line segments se approximate karte hain:

  1. Ideal model: Vγ=0, rf=0 (switch).
  2. Constant voltage drop model: ON → battery Vγ (0.7 V); OFF → open.
  3. Piecewise linear model: ON (V ≥ Vγ) → Vγ battery + rf series; OFF (V < Vγ) → open circuit. I = (V − Vγ)/rf.
Exam Tip Numericals mein jab kuch na diya ho to Si ke liye Vγ = 0.7 V, Ge ke liye 0.3 V lo. Example: VS = 10 V, R = 1 kΩ, Si diode, rf = 10 Ω → I = (10 − 0.7)/(1000 + 10) = 9.21 mA.

UNIT II Applications of Diode & Wave Shaping Circuits

1. Zener Diode & Design of Zener Regulator

Zener diode heavily doped P-N diode hai jo reverse breakdown region mein operate karne ke liye design hota hai. Breakdown mein current kaafi change hone par bhi voltage ≈ VZ constant rehta hai → voltage regulator / reference.

  • Parameters: VZ, IZ(min) (= IZK, knee), IZ(max) = PZ/VZ, zener resistance rZ.
Design Formulas RS = Vin − VZIZ + IL ;   RS(max) = Vin(min) − VZIZ(min) + IL(max) ;   RS(min) = Vin(max) − VZIZ(max) + IL(min) Zener ON rahe iske liye: VL = VinRL/(RS+RL) ≥ VZ. Zener power PZ = VZIZ ≤ PZ(max).

Example: Vin = 15 V, VZ = 10 V, IL = 20 mA, IZ(min) = 5 mA → RS = 5/25 mA = 200 Ω.

3. Power Rating of Diodes

Diode mein dissipated power PD = VD × ID. Maximum power rating PD(max) wo limit hai jiske upar junction temperature safe limit (Si ≈ 150–200 °C) se zyada ho jaata hai. Ambient temperature badhne par rating ko derate karte hain: PD(T) = PD(25 °C) − (derating factor)(T − 25). Other ratings: IF(max), PIV/PRV, surge current. Example: Zener 1 W, 5 V → IZ(max) = 200 mA.

5. Clippers (Limiters)

Clipper input waveform ke ek hissa (reference level ke upar ya neeche) ko kaat deta hai bina baaki shape badle. Types: series (diode load ke series mein) & shunt/parallel (diode load ke across); positive, negative, biased (battery VR ke saath) aur combination (double-ended) clipper (do diode + do battery, dono side clip). Shunt positive clipper: Vin > VR+Vγ → diode ON → Vo = VR+Vγ; else Vo = Vin.

6. Clampers (DC Restorers)

Clamper waveform ki shape same rakhkar usko DC level shift karta hai (C + diode + R). Capacitor peak tak charge hota hai aur fir battery ki tarah behave karta hai. Condition: τ = RC ≫ T/2.

  • Positive clamper: waveform upar shift; negative peak 0 par clamp → output 0 se 2Vm.
  • Negative clamper: waveform neeche shift → 0 se −2Vm.
  • Biased clamper: battery se clamping level VR par.

7. Voltage Multipliers

Diodes + capacitors se transformer ke peak Vm ka multiple DC output (high voltage, low current). Half-wave doubler: C1 Vm tak charge (D1), next half mein source + C1 series se C2 = 2Vm (D2 ka PIV = 2Vm). Full-wave doubler: C1, C2 alternate halves mein Vm charge, output series = 2Vm. Tripler/Quadrupler: aur stages jodkar 3Vm, 4Vm (n stages → nVm). Uses: CRT anode supply, X-ray, photocopier.

Exam Tip Clipper = shape change (part cut). Clamper = shape same, sirf DC level shift. Clamper mein capacitor zaroori, clipper mein nahi.

UNIT III BJT & FET

1. BJT Basic Concept

Bipolar Junction Transistor = 3 layer (NPN ya PNP), 2 junctions, 3 terminals: Emitter (heavily doped), Base (very thin, lightly doped), Collector (moderately doped, largest). "Bipolar" kyunki electrons aur holes dono conduction mein. Active mode: E-B junction forward, C-B junction reverse. Emitter se injected carriers ka ~98–99 % base cross karke collector tak pahunchta hai.

Formula IE = IB + IC α = IC/IE,   β = IC/IB,   β = α1−α,   α = β1+β, γ = 1+β IC = αIE + ICBO = βIB + (1+β)ICBO ICEO = (1+β)ICBO.

Biasing = DC voltages lagana taaki Q-point active region ke beech mein fix rahe (faithful amplification). Q-point temperature ke saath shift hota hai kyunki ICBO (har 10 °C par double), VBE (−2.5 mV/°C) aur β badalte hain. Stabilization = in changes ke bawajood IC constant rakhna.

5. AC & DC Load Line and Analysis

DC load line: VCE = VCC − IC(RC+RE). End points: (VCC, 0) aur (0, VCC/(RC+RE)). Slope = −1/RDC.

AC load line: AC mein capacitors short → Rac = RC‖RL (RE bypassed). Slope = −1/Rac (zyada steep), Q-point se pass hoti hai.

FormulaAC load line end points:IC(sat) = ICQ + VCEQ/RacVCE(cutoff) = VCEQ + ICQRacMaximum symmetrical swing ke liye Q-point AC load line ke centre par: VCEQ = ICQRac.

6. Compensation Techniques & Thermal Runaway

  • Thermal runaway: IC↑ → power dissipation PC = VCEIC↑ → junction temp Tj↑ → ICBO↑ → IC aur ↑ ... ye cumulative cycle transistor ko jala sakta hai.
  • Avoid karne ki condition: ∂PC/∂Tj < 1/θ (θ = thermal resistance, °C/W). Agar VCE < VCC/2 ho (RE wale circuit mein) to runaway nahi hota. Heat sink use karke θ kam karte hain.
  • Compensation techniques (stabilization se alag — temperature-sensitive devices se opposite effect): (i) Diode compensation for VBE: emitter/base circuit mein diode jiska VD bhi −2.5 mV/°C se change hota hai → VBE change cancel; (ii) Diode compensation for ICO: reverse biased diode ka leakage I0 base current se subtract; (iii) Thermistor (negative TC) R2 ki jagah ya RE ke saath; (iv) Sensistor (positive TC resistor).

7. Ebers-Moll Equations & Early Effect

Ebers-Moll Model (NPN) IE = IES(eVBE/VT − 1) − αRICS(eVBC/VT − 1) IC = αFIES(eVBE/VT − 1) − ICS(eVBC/VT − 1) Reciprocity: αFIES = αRICS (= IS). Model = do back-to-back diodes + do dependent current sources (αFIF, αRIR). Ye har region (active, saturation, cut-off) mein valid hai.

Early effect (base-width modulation): VCB (ya VCE) badhne par C-B depletion region chauda hota hai aur effective base width WB ghat jaati hai. Result: (i) base recombination kam → α, β badhte hain → IC VCE ke saath slightly badhta hai (output curves sloped); (ii) input curves shift; (iii) extreme case mein punch-through. Output curves ko peeche extend karne par wo −VA (Early voltage, ~50–200 V) par milte hain. Output resistance ro = VA/IC.

8. BJT as Amplifier & BJT as Switch

Amplifier (CE)

Q-point active region ke beech mein; small AC input base par → Ib change → Ic = βIb → RC par bada voltage change, 180° phase shift.

gm = IC/VT,   rπ = β/gm,   Av = −gm(RC‖RL) = −β(RC‖RL)/rπ

Example: IC = 1 mA → gm = 38.5 mA/V; RC = 4 kΩ → |Av| ≈ 154.

Switch

OFF: cut-off, Vin = 0 → IC ≈ 0, Vout = VCC (open switch). ON: saturation, VCE(sat) ≈ 0.2 V, IC(sat) = (VCC − VCE(sat))/RC (closed switch). Saturation ke liye IB > IC(sat)/β. Ye NOT gate (inverter) jaisa kaam karta hai; relays, LEDs, digital logic drive karta hai.

9. FET: Basic Concept

Field Effect Transistor = unipolar (sirf majority carriers), voltage-controlled device; gate voltage se channel ka width/conductivity control hota hai. Terminals: Source, Drain, Gate. Very high input impedance (JFET ~109 Ω, MOSFET ~1012–1015 Ω), low noise, thermally stable (no thermal runaway), chhota size.

JFET

N-channel bar ke dono side P+ gate regions. Gate-source junction reverse biased; VGS zyada negative → depletion chauda → channel patla → ID kam. VGS = VP (pinch-off / VGS(off)) par ID = 0.

  • Drain char. (ID vs VDS): ohmic region → pinch-off (saturation, ID const) → breakdown.
  • Transfer char. (ID vs VGS): parabolic (Shockley).

MOSFET

Gate SiO2 se insulated (IGFET). Depletion (D-MOSFET): channel pehle se bana; −VGS (depletion) aur +VGS (enhancement) dono mode. Enhancement (E-MOSFET): koi channel nahi; VGS > VT (threshold) par inversion layer channel banta hai; normally OFF.

Applications (JFET/MOSFET): buffer/ high-Z input amplifier, analog switch, chopper, VVR (voltage-variable resistor), RF amplifier, CMOS digital ICs, power switching (SMPS, motor drives), memory.

FET Formulas JFET / D-MOSFET: ID = IDSS(1 − VGS/VP)2;   gm = gm0(1 − VGS/VP), gm0 = 2IDSS/|VP|  |  E-MOSFET: ID = k(VGS − VT)2  |  CS amplifier Av = −gmRD.
Example: IDSS = 10 mA, VP = −4 V, VGS = −2 V → ID = 10(1−0.5)2 = 2.5 mA.

BJT vs FET: BJT bipolar, current-controlled, low–medium Zin, thermal runaway possible, higher gain-bandwidth; FET unipolar, voltage-controlled, very high Zin, thermally stable, less noise, smaller & easy IC fabrication.

UNIT IV Operational Amplifier

1. Op-Amp Basics

Op-amp = high-gain, direct-coupled differential amplifier IC (e.g. IC 741) jisme do inputs: inverting (−) aur non-inverting (+), ek output. Internal stages: differential amp → intermediate gain stage → level shifter → output (push-pull) stage. Vo = AOL(V2 − V1).

Virtual short: negative feedback ke saath V+ ≈ V− aur input current ≈ 0.

2. Inverting & Non-Inverting Modes

Op-Amp Gains Inverting: Av = VoVin = −RfR1,   Rin = R1 Non-inverting: Av = 1 + RfR1,   Rin very high Voltage follower (Rf=0, R1=∞): Av = 1 (buffer). Summer: Vo = −Rf(V1/R1+V2/R2+…).

Derivation (inverting): V− ≈ 0 (virtual ground). Current I = Vin/R1 = (0 − Vo)/Rf → Vo = −(Rf/R1)Vin. Non-inverting: V− = Vin = VoR1/(R1+Rf) → Vo = (1+Rf/R1)Vin. Example: R1 = 10 kΩ, Rf = 100 kΩ → inverting gain −10, non-inverting gain 11.

3. Comparators & Zero Crossing Detector

Comparator: op-amp open loop (no feedback) mein; ek input par signal, doosre par reference Vref. Vin > Vref (non-inverting comparator) → Vo = +Vsat; Vin < Vref → −Vsat (Vsat ≈ VCC − 1 to 2 V). Uses: level detector, ADC, PWM. Zero crossing detector (ZCD): comparator with Vref = 0; har baar input zero cross kare tab output state badalta hai → sine wave to square wave converter. Drawback: noisy input par zero ke paas multiple false triggering → solution = Schmitt trigger.

4. Schmitt Trigger (Regenerative Comparator)

Comparator + positive feedback (R1, R2 divider se output ka hissa + input par). Do threshold levels → hysteresis; noise immunity. Inverting Schmitt mein Vin −ve terminal par.

FormulaVUT = +VsatR2R1+R2,   VLT = −VsatR2R1+R2VH = VUT − VLT = 2VsatR2/(R1+R2)(R2 = feedback divider ka ground-side resistor.)

5. Instrumentation Amplifier

Low-level transducer signals (strain gauge, thermocouple) ko amplify karne ke liye high-gain, high input impedance, very high CMRR differential amplifier. Standard 3 op-amp circuit: do non-inverting buffer stages (A1, A2) jinke beech gain resistor RG, fir ek difference amplifier (A3). Gain sirf ek resistor RG badal kar adjust hota hai.

FormulaVo = R3R2(1 + 2R1RG)(V2 − V1)R1 = buffer feedback resistors; R2, R3 = difference-amp resistors. Features: low output impedance, low drift, gain accurately settable. Example IC: AD620, INA128.

6. 555 Timer & Its Applications

IC 555 (8-pin) = precision timing IC. Internal blocks: 3 equal 5 kΩ resistors divider (refs 2/3 VCC & 1/3 VCC), 2 comparators (upper/threshold, lower/trigger), SR flip-flop, discharge transistor, output buffer.

Pins: 1 GND, 2 Trigger, 3 Output, 4 Reset, 5 Control voltage, 6 Threshold, 7 Discharge, 8 VCC (4.5–18 V).

Applications: astable/monostable/bistable MV, PWM, PPM, missing pulse detector, frequency divider, LED flasher, timers, tone generator, ramp generator.

UNIT V CRO & Transducers

1. Cathode Ray Oscilloscope (CRO) — Construction

CRO ek instrument hai jo voltage waveform ko time ke saath screen par display karta hai; amplitude, time period, frequency, phase measure karte hain. Dil hai CRT (cathode ray tube) — evacuated glass tube with:

  • Electron gun: heater + cathode (thermionic emission), control grid (intensity/brightness — negative potential), pre-accelerating, focusing & accelerating anodes.
  • Deflection system: vertical (Y) plates aur horizontal (X) plates.
  • Fluorescent screen: phosphor coating (P31 green etc.); electron strike → light. Aquadag (graphite) coating secondary electrons collect karti hai.
  • Glass envelope: high vacuum, taaki electrons gas molecules se na takrayen.

2. Block Diagram of CRO

Delay line signal ko ~0.25 μs delay karti hai taaki sweep start hone ke baad signal Y plates par pahunche (leading edge dikh jaye). Time base sawtooth (ramp) voltage X plates par deta hai → spot left se right uniformly move karta hai, fir quick flyback (blanked). Trigger sweep ko signal ke same point se start karata hai → stable display.

3. Deflection & Focusing System

Electrostatic deflection

Plates ke beech field E = Vd/d electron ko parabolic path mein mod deta hai; plates ke baad seedha screen tak.

FormulaD = L l Vd2 d Va,   S = DVd = L l2 d Va m/VL = plates centre se screen distance, l = plate length, d = plate separation, Va = accelerating voltage. Deflection factor G = 1/S (V/m). Electron velocity v = √(2eVa/m).

Focusing

Electrostatic focusing: focusing anode aur accelerating anodes ke beech ka non-uniform field electrostatic lens banata hai jo divergent beam ko screen par ek sharp spot par converge karta hai (focus control = focusing anode potential adjust). Magnetic focusing (coil) TV CRTs mein. Magnetic deflection (coils) bhi possible: D ∝ 1/√Va, large-angle (TV), jabki CRO mein electrostatic (high frequency response).

Example: L = 20 cm, l = 2 cm, d = 0.5 cm, Va = 1000 V → S = (0.2×0.02)/(2×0.005×1000) = 0.4 mm/V.

6. Sampling & Storage Oscilloscopes

Sampling oscilloscope

Bahut high frequency (GHz) repetitive signals ke liye. Har cycle mein signal ka ek sample leta hai, har agle cycle mein sample point thoda aage shift (sequential sampling). Samples jodkar slow "reconstructed" waveform display hota hai. Condition: signal repetitive ho.

Storage oscilloscope

Waveform ko screen par lambe time tak store karta hai — slow/one-time transients ke liye. Analog storage: special CRT — bistable storage aur variable persistence (storage mesh + flood gun). Digital storage oscilloscope (DSO): signal → ADC → digital memory → DAC → display; waveform save, analyse, PC transfer.

7. Classification of Transducers

Transducer = device jo ek form ki energy/physical quantity (pressure, displacement, temp.) ko doosri form (mostly electrical) mein convert kare.

BasisTypes & Examples
PowerActive (self-generating, external power nahi): thermocouple, piezoelectric, photovoltaic cell. Passive (external excitation chahiye): strain gauge, LVDT, thermistor, RTD, capacitive.
PrincipleResistive (strain gauge, POT), Inductive (LVDT), Capacitive, Piezoelectric, Photoelectric, Thermoelectric
Prim./Sec.Primary: physical qty → intermediate (Bourdon tube, diaphragm). Secondary: intermediate → electrical (LVDT).

8. LVDT (Linear Variable Differential Transformer)

Passive inductive transducer jo linear displacement ko voltage mein badalta hai. Ek primary (centre) + do identical secondaries S1, S2 (series opposition); beech mein movable soft-iron core (shaft se juda). Primary par AC (1–10 kHz).

  • Null position (core centre): ES1 = ES2 → Eo = 0.
  • Core S1 ki taraf → ES1 > ES2, output in phase; S2 ki taraf → output 180° out of phase. Magnitude ∝ displacement (linear range), phase se direction.
LVDTEo = ES1 − ES2 ∝ xAdv: frictionless, high resolution & output, linear, rugged, low hysteresis. Disadv: stray magnetic field se affect, AC excitation + demodulator chahiye. Uses: displacement, force, pressure.

9. Strain Gauge

Passive resistive transducer. Fine wire/foil grid ko object par chipkate hain; strain se length badhti & area ghatta hai (piezoresistive effect se ρ bhi badalta hai) → resistance change. Δ R ko Wheatstone bridge se measure karte hain. Types: unbonded, bonded (wire/foil), semiconductor (G ≈ 100–200, temperature sensitive). Temperature compensation ke liye dummy gauge (unstrained) bridge ki adjacent arm mein.

Gauge FactorG = ΔR/RΔL/L = ΔR/Rε = 1 + 2ν + Δρ/ρεν = Poisson's ratio; metal gauge G ≈ 2. Example: R = 120 Ω, G = 2, ε = 500×10−6 → ΔR = GRε = 2×120×500×10−6 = 0.12 Ω.
