UNIT I Statics & Friction

1. Coplanar Forces & Resultant

Force ek vector quantity hai (magnitude, direction, point of application, line of action). Jab saari forces ek hi plane mein ho to unhe coplanar forces kehte hain.

TypeMeaningEquilibrium conditions
CollinearSab forces ek hi line of action parΣF = 0
ConcurrentSab forces ek point se pass hoti hainΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0
Parallel (like / unlike)Lines of action parallelΣFy = 0, ΣM = 0
Non-concurrent, non-parallel (general)Na ek point, na parallelΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0, ΣM = 0
Formula Resultant (resolution method): R = √[(ΣFx)2 + (ΣFy)2],   tan α = ΣFy / ΣFx Two forces P, Q at angle θ (parallelogram law): R = √(P2 + Q2 + 2PQ cos θ), tan α = Q sin θ / (P + Q cos θ).
Lami's theorem (3 concurrent forces in equilibrium): P/sin α = Q/sin β = R/sin γ (α = Q aur R ke beech ka angle, yaani P ke opposite wala angle; isi tarah β, γ).

2. Free Body Diagram (FBD)

FBD mein body ko surroundings se alag (isolate) karke uspar lagne wali saari forces dikhate hain: weight W (downward, CG par), applied loads, support reactions, friction, tension. Steps: (1) body isolate karo, (2) weight lagao, (3) har contact/support ko uske reaction se replace karo, (4) unknown directions assume karo (answer negative aaye to direction ulti).

Supports in FBD: smooth surface / roller → 1 reaction (surface ke normal); hinge / pin → 2 (Rx, Ry); fixed → 3 (Rx, Ry, M); string / cable → tension (body se door, pull).

3. Moment & Varignon's Theorem

Moment = Force × perpendicular distance (M = F·d), unit N·m. Clockwise / anticlockwise sign convention fix rakho. Couple = do equal, opposite, parallel forces → sirf rotation, moment = F × arm, kisi bhi point ke about same.

Varignon's Theorem Kisi point ke about forces ke system ke resultant ka moment = usi point ke about saari individual forces ke moments ka algebraic sum. R·d = ΣF·x. Use: resultant ki line of action (position) nikalne mein: d = ΣM / R.

Equilibrium conditions: Body equilibrium mein hai jab resultant force bhi zero aur resultant moment bhi zero: ΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0, ΣM = 0 (coplanar mein max 3 unknowns solve ho sakte hain). Two-force member: dono forces equal, opposite, collinear. Three-force member: forces concurrent ya parallel honi chahiye.

4. Polygon Law of Forces & Funicular Polygon (Graphical)

  • Polygon law: Agar kai concurrent forces ko magnitude + direction mein ek order mein (tip to tail) draw karein, to closing side ulti order mein resultant deti hai. Polygon close ho jaye → resultant zero → equilibrium (triangle law iska special case hai).
  • Bow's notation: Forces ke beech ke spaces ko A, B, C… se naam do; force ko do letters (AB) se denote karo — graphical work easy.
  • Funicular (link/string) polygon: Non-concurrent forces ke liye. Force polygon banao → ek pole O choose karo → rays (Oa, Ob…) draw karo → space diagram mein rays ke parallel links draw karo. Pehle aur last link ka intersection = resultant ki line of action.
  • Graphical equilibrium: Force polygon close → no resultant force; funicular polygon close → no resultant couple. Dono close → body in equilibrium. Beam ke support reactions isi se nikalte hain.

5. Analysis of Perfect Truss

Truss = straight members ka framework jo ends par pin-joint hote hain; load sirf joints par; members sirf axial force (Tension T ya Compression C) lete hain (self-weight neglect).

FormulaPerfect truss: m = 2j − 3  |  m < 2j − 3 → deficient  |  m > 2j − 3 → redundant (m = members, j = joints)

Method of Joints

  1. Pehle poore truss ke support reactions (ΣM, ΣFy, ΣFx).
  2. Aisa joint chuno jahan ≤ 2 unknown members ho.
  3. ΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0 lagao. Force joint se door → Tension; joint ki taraf → Compression.
  4. Joint-by-joint aage badho.

Method of Sections

  1. Reactions nikalo.
  2. Section line se truss ko kaato — max 3 unknown members cut hon (sab ek joint par na milte ho).
  3. Ek part ka FBD lo; ΣM about intersection of 2 unknowns → teesra directly.

Use: beech ke kuch members ki force jaldi chahiye ho to sections best.

Solved Symmetry se RA = RB = 5 kN.
Joint A: ΣFy: FAC sin60° = 5 → FAC = 5.77 kN (C).
ΣFx: FAB = FAC cos60° = 2.89 kN (T). Symmetry: FBC = 5.77 kN (C). Check m = 3, j = 3: 2(3)−3 = 3 → perfect.
Yaad Rakho Joint par sirf 2 non-collinear members aur koi load nahi → dono members ki force zero (zero-force members). 3 members, 2 collinear, no load → teesra zero.

6. Friction

Friction = contact surfaces ke beech relative motion ko oppose karne wali force. Static (limiting) > Dynamic (kinetic). Laws of Coulomb friction: F ∝ N; F direction motion ke opposite; F contact area par depend nahi karta; kinetic friction velocity se (lagbhag) independent.

FormulaF = μN,   μ = tan φ (φ = angle of friction),   angle of repose α = φ Resultant reaction S = √(N2 + F2), N ke saath angle φ banata hai.

(a) Friction on Inclined Plane (weight W, angle θ)

Effort P directionMoving UP the planeMoving DOWN the plane
Parallel to planeP = W(sin θ + μ cos θ)P = W(μ cos θ − sin θ)  (θ < φ)
HorizontalP = W tan(θ + φ)P = W tan(φ − θ)
At angle α to planeP = W sin(θ + φ) / cos(α − φ)P = W sin(φ − θ) / cos(α + φ)

Least effort to drag up: jab α = φ, tab Pmin = W sin(θ + φ). Example: W = 500 N, θ = 30°, μ = 0.2, P parallel up: P = 500(0.5 + 0.2×0.866) = 336.6 N.

(b) Screw Jack

Screw jack ek inclined plane hai jo cylinder par lipta hua hai. Thread ko unroll karo: base = πd, height = pitch p. Load W ko effort P (mean radius par) ghuma ke uthate hain.

Formula tan α = p / (πd);   P = W tan(α + φ);   Handle effort P1 = P·(d/2)/L η = tan α / tan(α + φ),   ηmax = (1 − sin φ)/(1 + sin φ);   Lowering torque = W tan(φ − α)·d/2 Multi-start thread: lead = n × pitch, tan α = lead/(πd). Self-locking (load khud neeche nahi aata) jab φ > α; tab η < 50%.

Example: d = 50 mm, p = 10 mm, μ = 0.1, W = 20 kN, handle L = 500 mm. tan α = 10/(π×50) = 0.0637 (α = 3.64°), φ = 5.71°. P = 20000 × tan 9.35° = 3294 N; handle effort = 3294×25/500 = 164.7 N; η = 0.0637/0.1647 = 38.6% → self-locking.

(c) Ladder Friction

Ladder (length l, weight W at middle) floor aur wall par tika hai. Ladder ka foot wall se door slip karta hai, isliye floor friction Ff wall ki taraf; top neeche slip karta hai, isliye wall friction Fw upar.

Equations: ΣFx = 0, ΣFy = 0, ΣMfoot = 0, with Ff = μfNf, Fw = μwNw (limiting).

Example: Smooth wall, floor μ = 0.25, ladder ka angle θ (floor se) on point of slipping? Nf = W, Nw = Ff = 0.25W. ΣM about foot: Nw·l sin θ = W·(l/2)cos θ → tan θ = 1/(2μ) = 2 → θ = 63.4°.

(d) Wedge Friction

Wedge ek chhota tapered block hai jo heavy load ko thoda uthane / adjust karne ke liye use hota hai. Effort P wedge par lagta hai; har contact surface par friction motion ke opposite. Method: wedge aur block ka alag-alag FBD banao; har surface par reaction R normal se φ angle par (Lami's theorem ya ΣFx, ΣFy). Pehle block ki equilibrium se unknown reaction, phir wedge se P.

Exam Tip Friction numericals mein sabse common galti friction ki direction hai — pehle socho body kis taraf move "karna chahti" hai, friction uske ulta lagao. Ladder, block-on-incline aur screw jack (η, self-locking) har saal aate hain.

4. Power Transmission — Belts & Ropes

Belts/ropes power ko ek shaft se doosre shaft tak friction ke through pahunchate hain. Types: flat belt (moderate distance, ~10 m tak), V-belt (chhoti distance, zyada grip, slip kam), circular rope (lambi distance, jaise cranes/mills). Drives: open belt (same direction rotation) aur cross belt (opposite direction).

Formula Velocity ratio: N2/N1 = d1/d2   [thickness t ke saath: (d1+t)/(d2+t); slip s% ke saath × (1 − s/100)] Length: Open L = π(r1+r2) + 2x + (r1−r2)2/x;   Cross L = π(r1+r2) + 2x + (r1+r2)2/x Angle of contact: Open θ = 180° − 2α, sin α = (r1−r2)/x;   Cross θ = 180° + 2α, sin α = (r1+r2)/x Ratio of tensions (flat): T1/T2 = eμθ;   V-belt/rope (groove angle 2β): T1/T2 = eμθ cosec β Power P = (T1 − T2)·v watt,   v = πdN/60 m/s Centrifugal tension Tc = mv2;   Max tension T = T1 + Tc = σ·b·t;   Max power when Tc = T/3, v = √(T/3m)

θ hamesha radian mein, aur smaller pulley ka angle of contact lo (open belt). T1 = tight side, T2 = slack side, m = mass per metre length. Initial tension T0 = (T1 + T2)/2 (+ Tc if considered).

Example Flat belt, μ = 0.3, θ = 180° = π rad, T1 = 1000 N, v = 10 m/s. T1/T2 = e0.3π = 2.566 → T2 = 389.7 N. P = (1000 − 389.7)×10 = 6.10 kW.

5. Gears & Gear Trains

Gears = toothed wheels; positive drive (no slip), exact velocity ratio, short centre distance, high power. Module m = d/T (mm), circular pitch pc = πd/T = πm.

Formula Two gears: N1/N2 = T2/T1 = d2/d1   (external mesh → opposite direction) Simple train: N1/Nn = Tn/T1 (idlers ratio change nahi karte, sirf direction; odd no. of idlers → same direction) Compound train: Nlast/Nfirst = (Product of teeth on drivers)/(Product of teeth on driven) = Train value
  • Simple gear train: har shaft par ek gear. Compound: ek shaft par do gears (badi reduction compact space mein). Reverted: compound train jismein first aur last gear co-axial (clock, lathe back gear); condition r1 + r2 = r3 + r4. Epicyclic: ek gear ka axis doosre ke around ghoomta hai (differential, hoist) — tabular method se solve.
  • Example (compound): A (20 T, driver, 1500 rpm) meshes B (60 T); C (25 T) B ke shaft par, meshes D (75 T). ND = 1500 × (20×25)/(60×75) = 166.7 rpm.

Belt vs Gear: belt mein slip → VR exact nahi, large centre distance, shock absorb, cheap; gear mein no slip → exact VR, small centre distance, high power, compact, par costly aur lubrication chahiye.

UNIT III Shear Force & Bending Moment

1. Types of Beams & Loading

Beam = structural member jo apni length ke perpendicular (transverse) load leta hai aur bending se resist karta hai.

Types of beams: cantilever (ek end fixed, doosra free), simply supported (dono ends par hinge/roller), overhanging (ek/dono end support ke bahar nikle) — ye teeno statically determinate (3 equations se reactions); fixed (dono ends fixed), propped cantilever (cantilever + free end par prop), continuous (2 se zyada supports) — indeterminate.

Types of loading: point/concentrated load W (kN) ek point par; UDL w (kN/m) — total wL, centre par act; UVL (triangular/trapezoidal) — triangle total wL/2, bade end se L/3 par act; applied couple/moment M (kN·m).

Definitions Shear Force (SF) at a section = section ke ek side (left ya right) ki saari vertical forces ka algebraic sum. Bending Moment (BM) at a section = section ke ek side ki saari forces ke moments ka algebraic sum (section ke about). Sign convention: Left side upward force → +SF; sagging (concave upward, beam "smile") → +BM, hogging → −BM.

2. Relationship between Load, SF and BM

FormuladF/dx = −w   (load intensity)    |    dM/dx = F   (shear force)
  • BM maximum wahan hota hai jahan SF = 0 (ya sign change karta hai).
  • No load portion → SF constant (horizontal line), BM linear (inclined line).
  • UDL → SF linear, BM parabolic (2nd degree). UVL → SF parabolic, BM cubic.
  • Point load par SF mein sudden jump (load ke barabar); applied couple par BM mein sudden jump.
  • Do sections ke beech BM ka change = SF diagram ka area: M2 − M1 = ∫F dx.

4. Overhanging Beam & Point of Contraflexure

Point of Contraflexure Woh point jahan BM sign change karta hai (sagging ↔ hogging), yaani BM = 0 (free/simple end ke alawa). Ye overhanging (aur fixed/continuous) beams mein milta hai, cantilever ya simple SS beam mein nahi. Wahan curvature ulti hoti hai — RCC mein reinforcement yahan bend/cut karte hain.

Example: Beam ABC, AB = 4 m (supports A, B), overhang BC = 1 m, UDL 2 kN/m over full 5 m.

Reactions: total load 10 kN at 2.5 m from A. ΣMA: RB×4 = 10×2.5 → RB = 6.25 kN, RA = 3.75 kN.

SF: A = +3.75; B (left) = 3.75 − 8 = −4.25; B (right) = −4.25 + 6.25 = +2.0; C = 0.

BM (0 ≤ x ≤ 4): M = 3.75x − x2. SF = 0 at x = 1.875 m → Mmax = +3.52 kN·m. MB = −2×12/2 = −1 kN·m.

Contraflexure: 3.75x − x2 = 0 → x = 3.75 m from A.

Exam Tip SFD/BMD steps: (1) reactions (ΣM = 0, ΣFy = 0) aur check, (2) har load point par SF, (3) SF = 0 wala point dhoondo → Mmax, (4) supports aur load points par BM, (5) curves ka shape (constant/linear/parabola) relationship se, (6) contraflexure M = 0 equation se. Values diagram par zaroor likho.

2. Bricks

Composition of good brick earth: silica 50–60% (shape), alumina 20–30% (plasticity), lime 5–10% (silica ko melt hone mein help, shrinkage kam), iron oxide 5–6% (red colour, strength), magnesia < 1%. Harmful: excess lime, alkalis (efflorescence), pebbles, iron pyrites, organic matter.

Manufacturing: preparation of clay → moulding (hand/machine) → drying → burning (clamp ya kiln — Bull's trench, Hoffman's) at ~1000 °C. Size: standard modular brick 190×90×90 mm (nominal with mortar 200×100×100 mm); traditional 9" × 4½" × 3".

3. Cement

OPC raw composition: lime CaO 60–67%, silica SiO2 17–25%, alumina Al2O3 3–8%, iron oxide 0.5–6%, MgO 0.1–4%, SO3 1–3%. Gypsum (~3%) setting retarder ke roop mein clinker grinding ke time add. Bogue compounds: C3S (early strength, 7 days), C2S (later strength), C3A (flash set, max heat), C4AF (little role). Manufacture: dry/wet process, rotary kiln ~1450 °C.

Types: OPC 33/43/53 grade (28-day strength in N/mm2), Rapid hardening (high C3S, early strength), Low heat (mass concrete — dams), Sulphate resisting (sea/sulphate soil), Portland Pozzolana Cement PPC (fly ash, durable), Portland slag cement, White cement (decoration), Quick setting (under-water), Hydrophobic, Coloured.

4. Lime

Limestone (CaCO3) ko jalane se quick lime CaO; paani milane (slaking) se hydrated / slaked lime Ca(OH)2. Hardening: CO2 absorb karke wapas CaCO3 (carbonation).

  • Fat (high calcium) lime: CaO > 95%, slaking vigorous, volume 2–2.5 times, sets slowly in air only — whitewash, plaster.
  • Hydraulic lime: clay 5–30%, paani mein bhi set — masonry mortar in damp places.
  • Poor (lean) lime: impurities > 30%, poor quality — inferior mortar.

Tests: visual, heat test, acid test (hydraulicity), ball test, workability. Uses: mortar, plaster, whitewash, soil stabilisation, sand-lime bricks.

5. Timber

Types: Softwood (conifers — pine, deodar, chir; light, straight grain) vs Hardwood (teak, sal, sheesham, oak; strong, durable). Structure: pith, heartwood (dark, strong), sapwood, cambium, bark, medullary rays.

Properties of good timber: uniform colour, straight fibres, sweet smell, high strength, durability, no knots/defects, moisture content ~10–12% (seasoned), sonorous.

Seasoning (moisture kam karna): natural air seasoning, water seasoning, artificial — kiln, chemical, boiling, electrical. Defects: knots, shakes (heart/cup/star), rot (dry/wet), twist, warp, cup, bow. Preservation: creosote, ASCU, coal tar, paint. Tests: moisture content, compression/tension/bending strength. Uses: doors, windows, frames, formwork, furniture, plywood.

6. Concrete & Mortar

Concrete = cement + fine aggregate (sand, < 4.75 mm) + coarse aggregate (> 4.75 mm, usually 10–20 mm) + water (+ admixtures). Mortar = binder (cement/lime) + sand + water — bricks/stones jodne aur plaster ke liye. Cement mortar 1:3 to 1:6 (masonry), 1:4–1:6 (plaster); lime mortar 1:2 / 1:3.

Workability

Fresh concrete ki ease of mixing, placing, compacting aur finishing without segregation. Depends on water content, w/c ratio, aggregate size/shape/grading, admixtures.

  • Slump test: frustum mould 300 mm high, 200 mm bottom, 100 mm top dia; 4 layers × 25 tamping; slump = height drop (25–75 mm normal RCC). Types: true, shear, collapse slump.
  • Compaction factor: CF = wt. partially compacted / wt. fully compacted (0.75–0.95); low workability ke liye accurate.
  • Vee-Bee consistometer (very stiff concrete, time in sec); flow table (high workability).

Strength Properties

  • Compressive strength: 150 mm cubes, 28-day curing; characteristic strength fck (5% results neeche gir sakte). Grade M20 = 20 N/mm2.
  • Abram's law: strength ∝ 1/(w/c ratio) — w/c badhao to strength ghatti. Usual w/c 0.4–0.6.
  • Tensile strength ~ 10–15% of compressive; flexural strength fcr = 0.7√fck (IS 456).
  • Other: durability, impermeability, shrinkage, creep, modulus Ec = 5000√fck.

7. Elements of Building Construction

(a) Foundations

Foundation (substructure) structure ka load safely soil tak pahunchata hai, settlement uniform rakhta hai. Shallow (depth ≤ width): spread, combined, strap, raft. Deep: pile, well, caisson.

  • Conventional spread footing: wall ya column ka base steps mein chauda karke load bade area par spread. Brick/stone masonry footing on lean concrete (PCC 1:4:8) bed; offset of each step ~ 50–60 mm. Wall footing (strip) aur isolated column footing (stepped/sloped).
  • RCC footings: isolated (single column), combined (2 columns paas-paas ya property line ke paas), strap (do footings beam se jude), raft/mat (poora area slab — weak soil, heavy loads). Steel tension zone (bottom) mein.
  • Minimum depth (Rankine): h = (p/γ)·[(1 − sin φ)/(1 + sin φ)]2; practically ≥ 0.9 m.

(b) Brick Masonry Walls

Bond = bricks ka arrangement jisse vertical joints continuous na ho. Stretcher = lambi face dikhe, header = chhoti face. Types: English bond (alternate courses of headers & stretchers; strongest, queen closer after first header), Flemish bond (har course mein header-stretcher alternate; better look; single/double Flemish), stretcher bond (half-brick partition walls), header bond (curved walls). Rules: bricks soak karke, joints ≤ 10 mm, lift ≤ 1.5 m/day, curing 7–10 days, plumb & level check.

(c) Plastering & Pointing

Plastering = wall/ceiling par mortar ki thin layer — smooth surface, protection from weather, appearance. Cement plaster 1:4 (external) / 1:6 (internal), thickness 12–20 mm (one/two coat). Types: lime, cement, mud, stucco, sand-faced, rough cast, pebble-dash. Defects: cracks, blistering, efflorescence, peeling.
Pointing = exposed masonry joints ko 10–20 mm rake karke rich mortar (1:2/1:3) se bharna — weather resistance + look. Types: flush, recessed, struck, V-grooved, weathered, tuck, beaded, rubbed.

(d) Floors

Floor ke parts: sub-grade (compacted soil), base course (PCC/brick bat), flooring/topping. Types: mud, brick, flagstone, cement concrete (IPS), terrazzo/mosaic, tiles (ceramic/vitrified), marble/granite/Kota stone, timber, PVC/linoleum. Selection: durability, cleanliness, cost, damp/sound/fire resistance, appearance.

(e) Roofs

Flat roofs (RCC slab, madras terrace, jack arch) — slope 1:100 drainage. Pitched roofs: lean-to/single (up to 2.5 m), couple (3.5 m), collar (4.5 m), king post truss (5–8 m), queen post truss (8–12 m), steel trusses (larger). Shell roofs (large spans). Covering: AC/GI sheets, tiles.

(f) Doors & Windows

Doors (min 2.0–2.1 m high, 0.9–1.0 m wide main): ledged, ledged-braced, ledged-braced-battened, framed & panelled, glazed, flush (most common), louvered, collapsible (steel, shops), rolling shutter (godowns), sliding, revolving (malls), swing.
Windows (glass area ~10–20% floor area): casement (side-hinged), double-hung/sash, pivoted, sliding, louvered, fixed, bay (projecting), dormer (sloping roof), clerestory, skylight, lantern, corner. Ventilator above for air.

(g) Lintels

Opening (door/window) ke upar horizontal member jo upar ki masonry ka load support karta hai. Types: timber, stone, brick (small span < 1 m), RCC (most common, precast/cast-in-situ), steel (I-section). Bearing = max(150 mm, lintel depth); depth ≈ span/12 (min 150 mm). Chhajja (weather shade) lintel ke saath cast. Arch = curved alternative.

(h) Staircases

Terms: tread (going), rise, nosing, flight (max 12–15 steps), landing, baluster, handrail, newel post, headroom (≥ 2.1 m). Good stair: rise 150–190 mm, tread 250–300 mm, 2R + T = 550–700 mm, width 0.9–1.0 m (residential), 1.5–2 m (public), pitch 25°–40°.

UNIT V Surveying & Positioning

Surveying = earth surface par points ki relative position (horizontal distance, direction, elevation) determine karke map/plan banana. Principles: (1) Whole to part — pehle main control framework, phir details (errors accumulate nahi hote), (2) naya point kam se kam do independent measurements se locate karo. Classification: plane vs geodetic (earth curvature ignore / consider); instrument wise — chain, compass, plane table, theodolite, levelling, tacheometric, total station.

Accessories: arrows (10 nos., mark chain lengths), ranging rods (2–3 m, red-white bands of 0.2 m), offset rods, pegs, plumb bob, line ranger, cross staff / optical square (right-angle offsets). Ranging = intermediate points ko line mein lana — direct (ends intervisible) / indirect (reciprocal, ends not visible). Chain survey: triangulation into well-conditioned triangles (angles 30°–120°); offsets (perpendicular/oblique). Errors: wrong chain length correction — True length = (L'/L) × measured length (L' = actual chain length, L = nominal).

(b) Levels

Horizontal line of sight dene wala instrument, elevations nikalne ke liye. Types: dumpy level (telescope fixed to vertical spindle — robust), tilting level, Wye (Y) level, automatic (auto) level (compensator), digital level. Parts: telescope, bubble tube, levelling head with foot screws, tripod. With levelling staff (self-reading — telescopic/folding 4 m; target staff). Temporary adjustments: (1) setting up, (2) levelling up (bubble centre — two foot screws parallel, phir third), (3) focusing eyepiece & object (elimination of parallax).

(c) Theodolite

Sabse precise angle-measuring instrument — horizontal & vertical angles, also prolonging lines, levelling, traversing. Parts: telescope, vertical circle, horizontal (upper & lower) plates, vernier (least count usually 20″), upper/lower clamps & tangent screws, plate levels, tribrach, trivet, plumb bob. Transit theodolite: telescope vertical plane mein 180° ghoom sakta hai. Terms: face left/face right, swinging, transiting, line of collimation. Temporary adjustments: setting up, centering, levelling, focusing. Methods: repetition (accurate single angle, vernier error kam), reiteration (several angles at a station). Face left + face right ka average instrumental errors cancel karta hai.

(d) Plane Table & Related Devices

Plane table survey: field mein hi plotting (graphical) — small scale maps, quick. Accessories: drawing board on tripod, alidade (plain/telescopic, sighting), spirit level, trough compass (orientation), plumbing fork + plumb bob (centering), drawing sheet. Setting: levelling, centering, orientation (by trough compass or back-sighting — most accurate). Methods: radiation (ek station se rays + distance), intersection (do stations se rays ka intersection — inaccessible points), traversing, resection (table ki khud ki position — two-point / three-point problem). Pros: no field book, fast; cons: wet weather mein mushkil, less accurate.
Related modern devices: EDM (electronic distance measurement), Total Station (electronic theodolite + EDM + microprocessor/data storage), GPS/GNSS (satellite positioning), digital levels, drones/LiDAR.

2. Measurement of Directions — Bearings & Compass

Bearing = kisi line ka horizontal angle ek reference (meridian) direction se. Meridians: true (geographical N-S, fixed), magnetic (compass needle direction), arbitrary. Magnetic declination = true & magnetic meridian ke beech angle: True bearing = Magnetic bearing ± declination (+ if E, − if W). Dip = needle ka horizontal se inclination.

  • WCB (Whole Circle Bearing): North se clockwise 0°–360° (prismatic compass).
  • QB / Reduced Bearing: N ya S se E ya W ki taraf 0°–90°, e.g. N 30° E (surveyor's compass).
  • Fore bearing (FB) = line ki direction of progress mein; Back bearing BB = FB ± 180° (FB < 180° → +, > 180° → −). QB mein: N↔S aur E↔W swap, angle same.
  • Included angle = difference of bearings of two lines meeting at a point.

Example: WCB 135° → S 45° E; 210° → S 30° W; 290° → N 70° W. FB of AB = 52° → BB = 232°.

Prismatic vs Surveyor's compass: prismatic mein graduated ring needle ke saath ghoomta hai, WCB, 0° at S end (inverted figures), prism se sighting + reading ek saath, tripod optional; surveyor's mein ring box ke saath fixed, needle ghoomti hai, QB, 0° at N & S (E-W interchanged), sighting aur reading alag, tripod essential.

Local attraction: nearby iron/electric cables needle ko deflect karte hain. Detection: kisi line ka FB − BB ≠ 180°. Correction: jis line ka FB − BB = 180° exactly ho, uske stations free maan kar baaki bearings correct karo (ya included-angle method). Other methods of measuring directions: theodolite se included angles / deflection angles (traverse), azimuth by astronomical observation, total station / GPS.

3. Measurement of Elevations — Levelling

Terms Datum = reference surface (MSL); Reduced Level (RL) = datum se point ki height; Bench Mark (BM) = known RL wala fixed point (GTS, permanent, arbitrary, temporary); Back Sight (BS) = known RL point par pehli reading after setting instrument; Fore Sight (FS) = instrument shift karne se pehle last reading; Intermediate Sight (IS) = beech ki readings; Change Point (CP) = jahan FS aur BS dono li jaati hain; Height of Instrument (HI) = line of collimation ka RL.
HI (Collimation) Method HI = RL of BM + BS;   RL = HI − (IS or FS)
Check: ΣBS − ΣFS = Last RL − First RL
Fast; IS ka check nahi — profile levelling ke liye.
Rise & Fall Method Prev. reading − current reading: +ve = Rise, −ve = Fall; RL = prev. RL + rise − fall
Check: ΣBS − ΣFS = ΣRise − ΣFall = Last RL − First RL
IS ka bhi check — accurate.

4. Reciprocal Levelling

Jab do points A, B ke beech river, valley ya pond ho (beech mein instrument nahi lag sakta), tab reciprocal levelling. Instrument pehle A ke paas lagao → readings a1 (A par), b1 (B par); phir B ke paas lagao → a2, b2.

FormulaTrue difference H = [(b1 − a1) + (b2 − a2)] / 2   (B, A se H neeche)Combined error e = [(b1 − a1) − (b2 − a2)] / 2 Ye method collimation error, curvature aur refraction (average) errors ko eliminate karta hai.

Example: a1 = 1.625, b1 = 2.545, a2 = 0.725, b2 = 1.405; RLA = 100.000. H = (0.920 + 0.680)/2 = 0.800 m → RLB = 99.200 m; error e = (0.920 − 0.680)/2 = 0.120 m.

Other levelling types: simple, differential/fly (door ke points, change points ke saath), profile (L-section) & cross-section, check levelling, trigonometric (vertical angles se), barometric. Curvature correction Cc = −0.0785 d2, refraction Cr = +0.0112 d2, combined = −0.0673 d2 (d in km, result in m).
