UNIT I Boilers & Thermodynamics of Steam

1. Boiler – Definition & Principal Parts

Definition Boiler (steam generator) ek closed vessel hai jisme fuel ke combustion se mili heat se water ko steam mein convert kiya jata hai, specified pressure aur temperature par (IBR Act ke according capacity > 22.75 litre).

Shell: cylindrical steel vessel, water + steam rakhta hai.

Furnace/Firebox & Grate: fuel jalane ki jagah; grate par solid fuel.

Fire tubes / Water tubes / Flue: hot gases aur water ke beech heat transfer path.

Water space & Steam space: neeche water, upar steam collect.

Chimney: draught paida karna, flue gases bahar. Ash pit: ash collect.

Brick/Refractory setting: gas passage banana, heat loss kam karna.

Classification: Fire-tube (gases tubes ke andar, water bahar) vs Water-tube (water tubes ke andar); internally vs externally fired; horizontal / vertical / inclined; stationary vs mobile; natural vs forced circulation; low / high pressure.

Cochran Boiler

  • Vertical cylindrical shell, hemispherical crown aur hemispherical firebox (max strength, min radiation loss).
  • Gases firebox → combustion chamber → horizontal smoke (fire) tubes → smoke box → chimney.
  • Kam floor area, portable, kisi bhi fuel ke liye suitable.

Locomotive Boiler

  • Firebox → bahut saare fire tubes → smoke box → chimney. Steam dome se steam li jati hai, superheater bhi hota hai.
  • Draught: engine ka exhaust steam blast pipe se chimney mein chhoda jata hai → forced draught. High steaming rate, compact.

Lancashire Boiler

  • Shell dia 2–3 m, length 7–9 m; do bade flue (fire) tubes jinke front end par grate.
  • Gas path: flue tubes (front→back) → bottom flue (back→front) → do side flues (front→back) → main flue → chimney. Is three-pass se heat transfer badhta hai.
  • Galloway tubes / dampers se draught control. Large water space → load fluctuation easily handle.

Babcock & Wilcox Boiler

  • Horizontal steam drum + water tubes ~10–15° incline par, uptake & downtake headers se connected.
  • Natural circulation: hot tubes mein water halka hokar uptake header se drum mein, thanda water downtake se wapas → continuous circulation. Baffles gases ko zig-zag path dete hain. Superheater tubes drum ke neeche.

3. Boiler Mountings & Accessories

Yaad Rakho Mountings = safety aur control ke liye, boiler par compulsory. Accessories = efficiency badhane ke liye, optional.
Mountings (safety/control):
  • Water level indicator – water level dikhata hai (2 per boiler).
  • Pressure gauge – steam pressure (Bourdon tube).
  • Safety valve – limit se zyada pressure par extra steam release (dead weight, lever, spring loaded, high-steam low-water).
  • Fusible plug – water level girne par low-melting alloy pighalta hai, steam furnace mein jaakar fire bujhati hai.
  • Steam stop valve – steam flow on/off. Feed check valve – feed water andar, wapas nahi (non-return).
  • Blow-off cock – sediment/mud nikalna. Manhole – inspection & cleaning.
Accessories (efficiency):
  • Economiser – flue gases se feed water preheat.
  • Air preheater – flue gases se combustion air preheat.
  • Superheater – saturated steam ko same pressure par superheat.
  • Feed pump – high pressure par water boiler mein bhejna. Injector – steam jet se feed water push (small/loco boilers).
  • Steam separator – steam se water particles alag. Steam trap – condensate drain bina steam loss.

4. Thermodynamics of Steam

Water ko constant pressure par heat karo: pehle temp saturation temp ts tak badhta hai, phir boiling (temp constant), end mein superheating.

Dryness fraction (x): wet steam mein dry steam ka mass fraction. x = 1 dry, x = 0 saturated water.

Sensible heat (hf): 0 °C water ko ts tak garam karne ki heat (temp change hota hai).

Latent heat (hfg): ts par saturated water ko dry saturated steam banane ki heat (temp constant). Pressure badhne par hfg ghatta hai (critical point 221.2 bar par zero).

Wet steam: steam + water droplets. Dry saturated: koi droplet nahi, ts par. Superheated: ts se upar temperature (tsup); degree of superheat = tsup − ts.

Internal energy (u): steam mein store energy = enthalpy − external work of evaporation (pv).

Formulas (per kg) x = msms + mw   |   Wet: h = hf + x·hfg,   v ≈ x·vg Dry sat.: h = hg = hf + hfg   |   Superheated: h = hg + cps(tsup − ts),   cps ≈ 2.1 kJ/kg·K vsup = vg·Tsup/Ts   |   Internal energy u = h − p·v   (p in kPa, v in m³/kg → kJ/kg)
Solved Example Steam at 10 bar, x = 0.9. Steam table: ts = 179.9 °C, hf = 763, hfg = 2015, hg = 2778 kJ/kg, vg = 0.1944 m³/kg.
h = 763 + 0.9×2015 = 2576.5 kJ/kg; v = 0.9×0.1944 = 0.175 m³/kg; u = 2576.5 − 1000×0.175 = 2401.5 kJ/kg.
Agar same pressure par 250 °C superheated: h = 2778 + 2.1(250 − 179.9) = 2925.2 kJ/kg.

UNIT II I.C. Engines & Laws of Thermodynamics

1. Internal Combustion Engine – Classification

IC engine mein fuel cylinder ke andar jalta hai (petrol, diesel engine); EC engine mein bahar (steam engine/turbine).

Fuel: petrol, diesel, gas (CNG/LPG), bi-fuel.

Ignition: Spark Ignition (SI) – spark plug; Compression Ignition (CI) – hot compressed air.

Strokes: four-stroke (2 rev/cycle), two-stroke (1 rev/cycle).

Cycle: Otto (const. volume), Diesel (const. pressure), Dual.

Cooling: air / water cooled. Cylinders: single/multi – inline, V, radial, opposed.

Application/speed: automobile, marine, stationary, aircraft; low/medium/high speed.

Terms: Bore (D), Stroke (L) = distance TDC→BDC, Swept volume Vs = (π/4)D²L, Clearance volume Vc, Compression ratio r = (Vs+Vc)/Vc.

2. Four-Stroke Petrol (SI) Engine

  1. Suction: piston TDC→BDC, inlet valve open; air–petrol mixture (carburettor/injector se) andar.
  2. Compression: dono valves band, piston BDC→TDC; mixture compress (r = 6–10).
  3. Power (expansion): TDC ke paas spark plug spark deta hai, mixture jalta hai, pressure badhta hai, piston neeche dhakka khata hai → work milta hai.
  4. Exhaust: exhaust valve open, piston upar aakar burnt gases bahar nikalta hai.

Ek cycle = 4 strokes = crankshaft ke 2 revolutions; sirf ek power stroke, isliye heavy flywheel chahiye.

3. Four-Stroke Diesel (CI) Engine

Same 4 strokes, par: suction mein sirf air; compression ratio high (14–22) se air temp ~600–700 °C ho jata hai; compression ke end par fuel injector diesel spray karta hai jo self-ignite hota hai (koi spark plug nahi). Phir power aur exhaust stroke.

4. Two-Stroke Engines

Valves ki jagah cylinder wall mein ports (inlet, transfer, exhaust) hote hain jinhe piston khud kholta/band karta hai. Crankcase sealed hota hai aur charge ka pre-compression karta hai. Har revolution mein ek power stroke.

  • Upward stroke (BDC→TDC): piston ke upar charge compress; piston ke neeche crankcase mein partial vacuum → inlet port khulta hai, fresh charge crankcase mein.
  • Downward stroke (TDC→BDC): ignition → expansion (power); end ke paas pehle exhaust port khulta hai (blow-down), phir transfer port se crankcase ka compressed charge cylinder mein aata hai aur burnt gases ko bahar dhakelta hai (scavenging, deflector-type piston crown).
  • 2-stroke petrol: crankcase mein air-fuel-lubricating oil mixture, spark plug. 2-stroke diesel: sirf air scavenge hoti hai (blower se), TDC par injector se fuel; badi marine engines.

7. Thermodynamics – Basic Concepts

System: space/quantity of matter jis par study focus hai; baaki surroundings; beech mein boundary (fixed ya movable).

  • Closed system: mass transfer nahi, energy (heat/work) transfer ho sakta hai – piston-cylinder gas.
  • Open system (control volume): mass + energy dono cross karte hain – turbine, compressor, nozzle.
  • Isolated system: na mass na energy – universe, ideal thermos flask.

Property: state ki measurable characteristic (p, V, T, U, H, S). Intensive – mass par depend nahi (p, T, density); Extensive – mass par depend (V, U, H). Property ek point function hai; heat aur work path functions hain.

State = properties ka set; Process = state change; Cycle = initial state par wapas aana; Quasi-static process = bahut dheere, har point equilibrium par. Thermodynamic equilibrium = thermal + mechanical + chemical equilibrium.

4. Psychrometry – Properties of Moist Air

Psychrometry = atmospheric air (dry air + water vapour) ki properties ka study; AC, cooling towers, drying mein use. Dalton's law: p = pa + pv (pv = vapour partial pressure).

UNIT IV Engineering Materials & Foundry

1. Classification of Engineering Materials

  • Metals: (a) Ferrous – iron base: cast iron, wrought iron, steels; (b) Non-ferrous – Al, Cu, Zn, Pb, Sn and alloys (brass = Cu+Zn, bronze = Cu+Sn).
  • Non-metals: polymers/plastics, ceramics (glass, refractory), rubber, wood; Composites (RCC, FRP, carbide tools).

Mechanical Properties

Strength: load sehne ki capacity bina failure (tensile, compressive, shear).

Elasticity: load hatane par original shape wapas.

Plasticity: permanent deformation bina tute (forging).

Ductility: tension mein wire mein kheencha ja sake (Cu, Al, mild steel).

Malleability: compression/hammering se sheet ban sake (gold, Al).

Toughness: fracture tak energy absorb karna (impact).

Hardness: scratch, indentation, wear resistance (Brinell, Rockwell).

Brittleness: bina deformation achanak tootna (cast iron, glass).

Stiffness: deformation resist karna (E high).

Resilience: elastic limit tak energy store (springs).

Fatigue: repeated/cyclic load mein yield se kam stress par failure.

Creep: high temp par constant load mein dheere-dheere permanent deformation.

Cast iron = iron + 2–4.5% carbon (theoretically 2.1–6.67%) + Si 1–3%, Mn 0.5–1%, S, P (impurities). Pig iron ko cupola mein remelt karke banta hai. Compressive strength high, tensile low, brittle, castability excellent, cheap.

4. Foundry (Casting)

Definition Casting = molten metal ko mould cavity mein daalkar solidify karwana jisse required shape mile. Jahan casting hoti hai use foundry kehte hain.

Basic steps: (1) Pattern making → (2) Mould & core making → (3) Melting (cupola for CI, crucible/induction furnace) → (4) Pouring (ladle se) → (5) Solidification & cooling → (6) Shake-out (mould todna) → (7) Fettling/cleaning (gates, risers, fins hatana) → (8) Inspection.

Equipment & tools: moulding boxes/flasks (cope – upper, drag – lower, cheek – middle), moulding board, shovel, riddle (sieve), rammer, strike-off bar, sprue pin, riser pin, vent wire, trowel, slick, lifter, draw spike, gate cutter, swab, bellows, ladle, cupola/crucible furnace, moulding machines (jolt, squeeze).

Patterns

Pattern = casting ka replica (thoda bada, allowances ke saath) jisse mould cavity banti hai.

Types:
  • Single piece (solid) – simple shapes.
  • Split – complex shapes; cope & drag mein half-half.
  • Loose piece – projections alag nikaalne layak.
  • Match plate – plate ke dono taraf; mass production.
  • Gated – gates/runners attached; multiple small castings.
  • Sweep – symmetric (bell, wheel) shapes, board ghuma kar.
  • Skeleton – large castings, frame type.
Materials:
  • Wood (teak, pine, mahogany) – cheap, easy, light; but moisture absorb, short life.
  • Metal (Al, CI, brass) – durable, accurate; mass production; costly.
  • Plastic / epoxy – light, smooth, moisture-proof.
  • Plaster of Paris – intricate shapes.
  • Wax – investment casting (pattern melt out).

Other types: cope & drag, follow board, segmental.

Moulding Sand – Types & Composition

Composition (typical green sand): silica sand (SiO₂) ~70–85% (refractoriness), clay/bentonite binder ~10–15% (strength), water ~4–8% (clay ko activate karta hai), additives (sea coal, cereal, wood flour, molasses) for surface finish & collapsibility.

Green sand: moist sand (silica + clay + water); sabse common, cheap.

Dry sand: green sand mould ko oven mein baked; strong, large castings.

Loam sand: ~50% clay; bade castings, sweep moulding.

Facing sand: pattern ke direct contact mein, fine, high quality.

Backing (floor) sand: purani reused sand, facing ke peeche.

Parting sand: clay-free dry sand; cope-drag ko chipakne se rokti hai.

Core sand: silica + oil binder (linseed); cores ke liye.

System sand: mechanised foundry mein poore mould ke liye.

Properties of good sand: permeability (gases nikal sake), refractoriness, cohesiveness (strength), adhesiveness, plasticity/flowability, collapsibility, reusability.

Mould Making (Green sand, two-box)

  1. Moulding board par drag box rakho, drag half pattern rakho, facing sand phir backing sand bhar ke ram karo, strike off, vent holes.
  2. Drag ko ulta karo, cope box rakho, cope half pattern lagao, parting sand chhidko.
  3. Sprue pin aur riser pin lagao, cope mein sand bhar ke ram karo, vent karo, pins nikalo, pouring basin banao.
  4. Cope uthao, pattern ko rap karke draw spike se nikalo, gate cut karo, mould repair, core rakho (agar hole chahiye).
  5. Cope wapas rakh ke clamp karo; ab mould pouring ke liye ready.

UNIT V Fabrication (Welding) & Machine Tools

1. Welding & Weldability

Definition Welding = do metal pieces ko heat (aur/ya pressure) se, filler metal ke saath ya bina, permanently join karna. Weldability = kisi metal ki wo capacity jisse use bina cracks/defects ke satisfactory weld kiya ja sake (low carbon steel – excellent; CI, high carbon steel – poor, preheat chahiye).

Classification of Welding Processes

Fusion welding (only heat, metal melts)Pressure / Solid-state welding (heat + pressure)
Gas welding (oxy-acetylene); Arc welding – SMAW, TIG, MIG, SAW, carbon arc; Thermit welding; Electron beam, Laser beamForge welding; Resistance welding – spot, seam, projection, butt, flash; Friction welding; Ultrasonic, Explosive welding

Edge Preparation & Types of Joints

Edge preparation zaruri hai taaki full penetration ho. Square (thin), single-V, double-V, single/double-U, J, bevel. Types of joints: Butt (same plane, end to end), Lap (overlap), Tee (90° T-shape), Corner (L-shape, box corners), Edge (parallel sheets ke edges). Welds: fillet weld (lap, tee, corner), groove weld (butt).

2. Gas Welding (Oxy-Acetylene)

Oxygen + acetylene (C₂H₂) jalane se ~3200 °C flame; filler rod aur flux se joint. Equipment: O₂ cylinder (black), C₂H₂ cylinder (maroon, acetone mein dissolved), pressure regulators with gauges, hoses (O₂ – black/green, C₂H₂ – red), welding torch (blow pipe) with nozzles, filler rods, flux, spark lighter, goggles, gloves.

FlameO₂ : C₂H₂FeatureUse
Neutral≈ 1 : 1~3200 °C, sharp inner cone, no chemical effectMost metals – MS, CI, Cu, Al, SS
Carburising (reducing)Excess C₂H₂Feather (acetylene) zone, adds carbonHigh carbon steel, hard facing, Monel

Techniques: Leftward (forehand) – thin plates (< 5 mm); Rightward (backhand) – thick plates. Gas cutting = oxy-acetylene se preheat + O₂ jet se iron oxidise.

3. Arc Welding

Electrode aur workpiece ke beech electric arc (~4000–6000 °C) metal pighlata hai. Equipment: power source – AC welding transformer ya DC generator/rectifier (OCV ~50–90 V, arc voltage ~20–40 V, current 50–500 A), electrode holder, welding cables, earth (ground) clamp, helmet/hand shield with dark glass, chipping hammer, wire brush, gloves, apron.

Electrodes

  • Consumable: melt hokar filler banta hai – bare, lightly coated, heavily coated (shielded). Mild steel core wire + flux coating.
  • Non-consumable: carbon, graphite, tungsten (TIG) – pighalte nahi.
  • Flux coating ke kaam: arc stabilise, shielding gas paida karna (oxidation se bachaav), slag banana (slow cooling, protection), alloying elements add karna, impurities hatana.

Arc Welding Processes

SMAW (MMAW, stick): flux-coated consumable electrode, manual; general fabrication.

TIG (GTAW): non-consumable tungsten electrode + argon/helium shield, alag filler; Al, SS, thin sheets, high quality.

MIG (GMAW): continuously fed consumable wire + inert gas (Ar/CO₂); fast, semi-automatic.

SAW (Submerged arc): arc granular flux ke neeche chhupa; thick plates, long straight seams, automatic.

Carbon arc: carbon electrode, filler alag. Resistance spot: I²Rt heat + pressure; sheet metal, car bodies.

Gas vs Arc: gas – flame ~3200 °C, wide HAZ, more distortion, thin sheets, slow, portable (no electricity); arc – ~4000–6000 °C, narrow HAZ, thick plates, faster & stronger joint, needs power supply.

6. Machine Tools

Machine tool = power driven machine jo cutting tool se workpiece ka extra metal (chips) hata kar required shape, size, finish deta hai. Basic need: work aur tool ko hold karna, relative motion (cutting + feed) dena. Cutting speed V = πDN/1000 m/min (D in mm, N in rpm). Example: D = 50 mm, N = 400 rpm ⇒ V = 62.8 m/min. Lathe machining time T = L/(f·N) min.

(a) Lathe – "Mother of all machine tools"

Parts: Bed (guideways), Headstock (spindle, gear box, chuck), Tailstock (dead centre, drill holding), Carriage = saddle + cross slide + compound rest + tool post + apron, feed rod, lead screw (threading), legs.

Specification: height of centres, swing over bed (max dia), distance between centres (max job length), bed length, spindle bore, no. of speeds, motor power. e.g. "Lathe 1500 × 300 mm".

Tools: single-point cutting tools (HSS, carbide-tipped) – turning, facing, parting, threading, boring tools, knurling tool. Operations/Applications: facing, plain & step turning, taper turning (compound rest, tailstock set-over, taper attachment, form tool), thread cutting, drilling, boring, reaming, knurling, chamfering, grooving, parting-off. Shafts, bolts, pulleys, cylindrical jobs.

(b) Drilling Machine

Types: portable, sensitive (bench) – small holes up to ~15 mm, hand feed; upright (pillar/column) – medium jobs; radial – heavy jobs, arm column ke around ghoomta hai; gang, multi-spindle. Parts: base, column/pillar, table, radial arm (radial), drill head, spindle, chuck/sleeve, feed handle, motor. Specification: max drill dia, spindle travel, table size, distance spindle to column/table, (radial: arm length, column dia), speeds, power. Tools: twist drill (point angle 118°, HSS), reamer, counterbore, countersink, tap. Operations: drilling, reaming, boring, counterboring, countersinking, spot facing, tapping, trepanning.

(c) Shaper

Single-point tool ram par reciprocate karta hai; work table par fixed, har return stroke ke baad feed. Forward stroke = cutting, return idle – quick return mechanism (crank & slotted lever) se return fast (cutting : return time ≈ 3 : 2). Parts: base, column/body, ram, tool head (clapper box, tool post, down-feed screw), table, cross rail, saddle, drive mechanism. Specification: max stroke length (e.g. 600 mm), table size, max travel, power, ratio of cutting to return time. Applications: flat horizontal, vertical, inclined surfaces, keyways, slots, grooves, splines.

(d) Milling Machine

Multi-point rotating cutter, work table par feed hota hai; high metal removal, accurate. Types: column & knee – horizontal, vertical, universal (table swivel, helical milling); planer type, bed type. Parts: base, column, knee (vertical move), saddle (cross), table (longitudinal), spindle, arbor, overarm, dividing (indexing) head. Specification: table working surface, longitudinal/cross/vertical travel, spindle speeds & feeds, power, spindle nose taper. Cutters: plain (slab), side & face, end mill, face mill, slot/slitting saw, angle, form, gear cutter. Up milling (conventional – cutter rotation feed ke opposite) vs down milling (climb – same direction, better finish, needs backlash eliminator). Operations: plain/face/angular milling, slotting, keyway, gear cutting, straddle & gang milling.

(e) Grinding Machine

Rotating abrasive wheel se bahut kam metal hata kar high accuracy & fine finish (hard materials bhi). Types: surface (flat), cylindrical (external – between centres), internal (holes), centreless (no centres; regulating wheel – pins, rods mass production), tool & cutter grinder, bench/pedestal grinder. Parts: base, table, work head, tailstock, wheel head, wheel guard, coolant system. Specification: max dia & length of job, wheel size, table size, power. Wheel: abrasive – Al₂O₃ (steels), SiC (CI, non-ferrous, carbide), diamond, CBN; grain size, grade (A soft–Z hard), structure, bond (V vitrified, B resinoid, R rubber, E shellac, S silicate, M metallic). Marking e.g. 51 A 46 K 5 V 23. Dressing = clogged wheel ko sharp karna; truing = wheel ko shape/round mein lana.
