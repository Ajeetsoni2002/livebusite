UNIT I Quantum Physics

1. Phase Velocity, Group Velocity aur unka Relation

de Broglie: har moving particle ke saath matter wave, λ = h/p = h/mv. Particle ko represent karne ke liye kai waves superpose karke wave packet banate hain.

Phase velocity Constant phase point ki speed: vp = ω/k. Matter wave: vp = E/p = c²/v > c (no energy carry, physically meaningless).
Group velocity Wave packet (envelope) ki speed: vg = dω/dk. Matter wave: vg = v (particle velocity) — energy isi se travel.
  • y₁ = A sin(ωt − kx), y₂ = A sin[(ω+Δω)t − (k+Δk)x] add karo ⇒ y = 2A cos(Δωt/2 − Δkx/2)·sin(ωt − kx); envelope speed = Δω/Δk → dω/dk.
  • E = ħω, p = ħk ⇒ vg = dE/dp; E = p²/2m ⇒ vg = p/m = v. Relation: ω = kvp ⇒ vg = vp + k dvp/dk.
Formulavg = vp − λ (dvp/dλ)  ;  vp · vg = c² (relativistic particle)Non-dispersive medium (dvp/dλ = 0) ⇒ vg = vp; normal dispersion ⇒ vg < vp.

2. Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

Statement Particle ki position aur momentum ek saath exactly measure karna impossible: Δx · Δpx ≥ ħ/2 (elementary form Δx·Δp ≈ h). Isi tarah ΔE·Δt ≥ ħ/2, ΔL·Δθ ≥ ħ/2.

Elementary proof: k aur k+Δk waves ke superposition se ek group ki length Δx = 2π/Δk. p = ħk ⇒ Δp = ħΔk ⇒ Δx·Δp = 2πħ = h. Packet chhota (Δx kam) ⇒ Δp zyada.

(a) Gamma-ray microscope se electron ki position

  • Resolving limit: Δx = λ/(2 sin θ) (θ = semi-vertical angle).
  • Scattered photon (h/λ) cone 2θ mein kahin bhi ⇒ recoil Δpx = 2(h/λ) sin θ.
  • ⇒ Δx·Δpx = h. Chhota λ ⇒ Δx kam par Δp zyada.

(b) Electron nucleus mein nahi: Nucleus dia ~ 2×10−14 m = Δx ⇒ Δp ≈ h/Δx ≈ 3.3×10−20 kg m/s ⇒ E ≈ pc ≈ 10−11 J ≈ 60 MeV. Par β-decay electrons sirf 2–4 MeV ke hote hain ⇒ electron nucleus mein exist nahi kar sakta.

(c) Single slit se electron diffraction: Slit width d = Δy. First minimum d sinθ = λ. y-momentum −p sinθ se +p sinθ ⇒ Δpy = 2p sinθ = 2h/d ⇒ Δy·Δpy = 2h ≥ ħ/2. Narrow slit ⇒ zyada spread.

3. Compton Scattering

X-ray (λ) free electron se scatter hone par wavelength badh jaati hai (λ′ > λ) — light ke photon (particle) nature ka proof.

Derivation: Energy: hν + m₀c² = hν′ + mc². Momentum: h/λ = (h/λ′)cosφ + p cosθ, 0 = (h/λ′)sinφ − p sinθ. θ eliminate karo + E² = p²c² + m₀²c⁴ ⇒

Compton shiftΔλ = λ′ − λ = hm₀c(1 − cos φ)h/m₀c = 0.02426 Å (Compton wavelength). φ = 90° ⇒ 0.0243 Å; φ = 180° ⇒ Δλmax = 0.0485 Å. Numerical: λ = 1 Å, φ = 60° ⇒ Δλ = 0.0121 Å, λ′ = 1.0121 Å. Δλ λ pe depend nahi karta ⇒ visible light mein negligible.

  • ∂ψ/∂x continuous (finite V pe).
  • ψ → 0 as x → ±∞ (bound states).
  • Orthogonal: ∫ψm*ψn dx = 0 (m ≠ n).
  • Aisa ψ = well-behaved wave function.

6. Particle in 1-D Infinite Square Potential Well

V = 0 (0 < x < L), V = ∞ bahar ⇒ bahar ψ = 0. Andar: d²ψ/dx² + k²ψ = 0, k² = 2mE/ħ².

  • ψ = A sin kx + B cos kx; ψ(0) = 0 ⇒ B = 0.
  • ψ(L) = 0 ⇒ sin kL = 0 ⇒ k = nπ/L, n = 1,2,3…
  • En = ħ²k²/2m = n²h²/8mL².
  • Normalize: A²L/2 = 1 ⇒ A = √(2/L).
ResultEn = n²h²8mL²  ;  ψn(x) = √(2/L) sin(nπx/L)n = 0 nahi (ψ ≡ 0) ⇒ zero-point energy E₁ ≠ 0. Energy quantized, E ∝ n², E ∝ 1/L². Numerical: electron, L = 1 Å ⇒ E₁ = (6.63×10−34)²/(8×9.1×10−31×10−20) = 6.03×10−18 J = 37.7 eV; E₂ = 150.8 eV.

UNIT II Wave Optics

Interference: do coherent sources (same frequency, constant phase difference) ki waves ka superposition. Path diff. Δ = nλ ⇒ bright; Δ = (2n+1)λ/2 ⇒ dark; phase diff δ = (2π/λ)Δ.

Do thin prisms (angle ~0.5°) base-to-base. Slit S se do virtual coherent sources S₁, S₂ (separation d) — division of wavefront.

2. Nuclear Shell Model

Atomic shells ki tarah nucleons average central potential mein independently move karte hue discrete shells bharte hain (p aur n alag, Pauli principle). Magic numbers 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126 pe nuclei extra stable (high B/A, zyada isotopes, low n-absorption) — e.g. ₂He⁴, ₈O¹⁶, ₂₀Ca⁴⁰, ₈₂Pb²⁰⁸ (doubly magic). Strong spin–orbit coupling (Mayer–Jensen) se j = l ± ½ splitting magic numbers deta hai. Success: magic numbers, ground state spin–parity, magnetic moments, isomerism. Failure: fission, large quadrupole moments.

3. Particle Accelerators

(a) Linear Accelerator: Coaxial drift tubes, alternate tubes RF source ke opposite terminals pe. Tube ke andar field zero (drift), gaps mein acceleration. Har tube mein time T/2 rakhne ke liye length badhti: Ln = (1/2f)√(2nqV/m) ∝ √n; KE after n gaps = nqV.

(b) Cyclotron: Do hollow dees strong uniform B mein, beech mein HF AC. Dee ke andar semicircle (field-free), gap mein acceleration; spiral path.

  • r = mv/qB; semicircle time πm/qB — v, r se independent.
  • Resonance: f = qB/2πm; Emax = q²B²R²/2m.
  • Limits: relativistic mass increase ⇒ out of phase (p ~25 MeV max); electrons & neutrals nahi.
Numerical Proton, B = 1.5 T: f = (1.6×10−19×1.5)/(2π×1.67×10−27) ≈ 22.9 MHz. R = 0.5 m ⇒ Emax = (1.6×10−19×1.5×0.5)²/(2×1.67×10−27) = 4.31×10−12 J ≈ 27 MeV.
  • (c) Synchrocyclotron (FM cyclotron): B constant, oscillator frequency time ke saath ghatate hain (f = qB/2πm, m badhta) ⇒ relativistic problem solved. Beam pulsed; protons ~700 MeV.
  • (d) Synchrotron: Particles fixed radius ring (doughnut) mein; energy ke saath B aur f dono badhate hain (r = p/qB constant). Ring ke around bending magnets + RF cavities. Proton synchrotron (GeV–TeV, LHC); electron synchrotron mein synchrotron radiation loss limit.
  • (e) Betatron (Kerst, electrons): Doughnut chamber, fixed orbit r₀; acceleration changing flux se induced E-field (transformer, electron = secondary). Energy/rev = e dφ/dt. Betatron (2:1) condition: Borbit = ½ Bavg inside, i.e. φ = 2πr₀²B0. AC ke first quarter cycle mein hi acceleration; ~100–300 MeV; X-rays, therapy.

4. Geiger–Müller Counter

Metal tube (cathode) + axial thin wire (anode, ~ +1000 V). Gas: argon + quenching agent (alcohol/halogen) at low pressure. Radiation gas ionize karti → electrons anode ki taraf accelerate → avalanche (Townsend) poore wire pe ⇒ large pulse (initial ionization se independent) → counter. Quencher continuous discharge rokta; dead time ~ 100–200 μs. Operating voltage = plateau region. β ~100% efficient, γ ~1%; particle type/energy nahi batata.

5. Motion in Crossed Electric & Magnetic Fields

F = q(E + v × B), E ⊥ B. Velocity selector: qE = qvB ⇒ v = E/B wale particles undeflected (Wien filter, Thomson e/m). Rest se start ⇒ cycloidal path, drift velocity vd = E/B along E × B (q, m se independent). Only B ⇒ circle (r = mv/qB); only E ⇒ parabola.

6. Mass Spectrographs

(a) Bainbridge: Ions slits se → velocity selector (E ⊥ B₁), sirf v = E/B₁ pass → chamber mein B₂ ⇒ semicircle → photographic plate.

r = mv/qB₂ = mE/(qB₁B₂) ⇒ m = qB₁B₂r/E ∝ r

⇒ linear mass scale; isotopes: Δm = qB₁B₂Δr/E.

(b) Aston: Ion beam pehle electric field se ek taraf deflect, phir magnetic field se opposite taraf. Same e/m (different v) wale ions plate pe ek point pe focus — velocity focusing; different masses ⇒ alag lines, approx. linear scale. Aston ne isotopes (Ne-20, Ne-22) aur whole number rule diya; accuracy ~1 in 10⁴. Bainbridge = velocity selection (exactly linear scale); Aston = velocity focusing.

UNIT IV Solid State Physics

1. Kronig–Penney Model (Qualitative)

Crystal ka periodic potential = 1-D periodic rectangular wells (barrier V₀, width b, period a+b). Schrödinger eq. + Bloch theorem se:

P (sin αa)/(αa) + cos αa = cos ka ,  P = mV₀ba/ħ²

  • cos ka sirf −1 se +1 ⇒ LHS jahan is range mein ho wahi E allowed ⇒ allowed bands + forbidden gaps.
  • E badhe ⇒ band width badhe. P → ∞: discrete levels; P → 0: free electron.
  • E–k mein gaps at k = nπ/a (Brillouin zone edges).

2. Effective Mass

Crystal mein lattice ke internal force ka effect ek modified mass mein daalte hain taaki Fext = m*a: m* = ħ² / (d²E/dk²). Band bottom pe m* > 0; band top pe m* < 0 ⇒ hole (positive charge, positive mass) concept; inflection pe m* → ∞. Narrow band ⇒ heavy m*.

3. Fermi–Dirac Distribution

F-D functionf(E) = 11 + e(E − EF)/kT
  • f(E) = level E ke occupied hone ki probability.
  • T = 0: E < EF ⇒ f = 1, E > EF ⇒ f = 0.
  • T > 0: E = EF pe f = ½. Fermi level = ½ occupancy level (0 K pe highest filled).
  • E − EF ≫ kT ⇒ Maxwell–Boltzmann.

3. Active Medium, Population Inversion, Pumping, Resonator

  • Active medium: material jismein inversion aur lasing hoti (Cr³⁺ in ruby, Ne in He-Ne, CO₂).
  • Population inversion: N₂ > N₁ (normally N₂ < N₁). Metastable state (τ ~10−3 s vs 10−8 s) zaroori; 2-level mein impossible, 3/4-level scheme use (4-level easier).
  • Pumping: optical (flash lamp — ruby, Nd:YAG), electrical discharge (He-Ne, CO₂), chemical, current injection (diode laser).
  • Optical resonator: do mirrors (100% + partial ~99%) ⇒ feedback, amplification, sirf axial photons survive; L = nλ/2 (modes). Types: plane-parallel, confocal.

4. Characteristics of Laser Beam

  • Coherence: temporal (lc = cτc, He-Ne ~ km tak) & spatial.
  • Directionality: nearly parallel beam.
  • Divergence: θ = (r₂ − r₁)/(d₂ − d₁) ≈ 1.22λ/D, ~mrad.
  • Monochromatic (Δλ ~10−3 nm) & high intensity.

7. Acceptance Angle, Cone, NA, V-number (Ray Theory)

Ray axis se θ₀ pe enter, core mein θr, interface pe (90° − θr) ≥ θc chahiye (TIR, zig-zag propagation).

  • μ₀ sin θ₀ = μ₁ sin θr = μ₁ cos θc (limit).
  • cos θc = √(1 − μ₂²/μ₁²) ⇒ μ₀ sin θmax = √(μ₁² − μ₂²).
  • Acceptance angle θmax; isse axis ke around ghumao ⇒ acceptance cone (full 2θmax).
NA & V-numberNA = μ₀ sin θmax = √(μ₁² − μ₂²) ≈ μ₁√(2Δ),  Δ = (μ₁ − μ₂)/μ₁  ;  V = (2πa/λ)·NAModes ≈ V²/2 (step), V²/4 (graded). V < 2.405 ⇒ single mode. Numerical: μ₁ = 1.50, μ₂ = 1.47 ⇒ NA = √0.0891 = 0.298, θmax = 17.3°, Δ = 0.02. a = 25 μm, λ = 1.3 μm ⇒ V ≈ 36, modes ≈ 648.

8. Pulse Dispersion, Attenuation & Losses

Dispersion = pulse ka broadening ⇒ overlap ⇒ bit rate limit. Intermodal (multimode, different path lengths): step index Δt ≈ Lμ₁Δ/c; graded ≈ Lμ₁Δ²/8c. Intramodal/chromatic: material (μ depends on λ) + waveguide dispersion.

Attenuation: α (dB/km) = (10/L) log₁₀(Pin/Pout); silica min ~0.2 dB/km at 1.55 μm (windows 0.85, 1.3, 1.55 μm). Losses: absorption (intrinsic UV/IR, impurities OH⁻, Fe, Cu), Rayleigh scattering ∝ 1/λ⁴, macro/micro bending, splice/connector/coupling losses. Numerical: 1 mW, 10 km, 0.5 dB/km ⇒ 5 dB ⇒ Pout = 10−0.5 mW = 0.316 mW.

9. Applications of Lasers & Optical Fibers

Lasers: cutting, welding, drilling (CO₂, Nd:YAG); eye surgery (LASIK, retina), bloodless surgery, cancer therapy; fiber communication source, holography, barcode, CD/DVD, printers; LIDAR, range finding, spectroscopy, isotope separation.
Optical fibers: telecom & internet (high bandwidth, low loss, no EMI, secure, light); medical endoscopy, laser delivery; sensors (temperature, pressure, strain); LAN, cable TV, defence/aircraft, decorative lighting.
