UNIT I Introduction, Scales, Conics & Special Curves

1. Engineering Drawing – Need & Classification

Definition Engineering drawing ek graphical language hai jisme kisi object ka shape, size, material aur manufacturing detail lines, symbols aur notes se exact tarike se dikhaya jata hai. Isko "language of engineers" kehte hain – har country ka engineer same convention padh leta hai.

Need: idea ko accurately communicate karna, manufacturing / construction ke liye exact size dena, records maintain karna, design check karna aur ambiguity khatam karna (words se 3D shape describe karna mushkil hai).

BasisTypes

FieldMachine drawing, Civil / building drawing, Electrical & electronic drawing, Structural, Production drawing
MethodFreehand sketch, Instrument drawing, Computer aided drawing (CAD)
ProjectionOrthographic (multi-view) vs Pictorial (isometric, oblique, perspective)

2. Drawing Instruments & Uses

Drawing board – soft wood board, sheet fix karne ke liye (D0–D3 sizes).

Mini-drafter – T-square + set-squares + protractor ka kaam ek saath; parallel lines kisi bhi angle par.

T-square – horizontal parallel lines; working edge par set-square slide hota hai.

Set-squares – 45° and 30°–60°; vertical / inclined lines (15° multiples).

Protractor – angles measure/mark karna.

Compass & divider – compass se circles/arcs; divider se distance transfer / line ko equal parts mein divide karna.

Bow compass / bow divider – chhote circles (dia < 25 mm) ke liye.

Scales – plastic/cardboard scales M1–M8 (e.g. M1 = 1:1 & 1:2.5).

French curves – irregular smooth curves (conics, cycloid) draw karne ke liye.

Pencils – grades: 2H/H (construction, thin lines), HB (lettering, outlines). H = hard, B = black/soft.

Others – eraser, erasing shield, drawing clips/pins, sand paper block, templates.

5. Technical Lettering

  • Single-stroke capital letters, vertical ya inclined (75° to horizontal).
  • Type A: stroke thickness d = h/14 (thin, condensed). Type B: d = h/10 (most used in schools).
  • Standard heights h: 2.5, 3.5, 5, 7, 10, 14, 20 mm. Drawing no./title: 7–10 mm; sub-titles: 5–7 mm; dimensions/notes: 3.5–5 mm.
  • Spacing between characters ≈ 2d (Type B: 0.2h), words ≈ 6d, lines ≈ 14d (baseline to baseline). Guide lines thin (2H) lagao.

6. Dimensioning

Dimensioning = drawing par size aur location ke numerical values + notes dena taaki part bina scale lagaye ban sake.

Elements: extension (projection) line – object se ~1 mm gap, dimension line se ~2–3 mm aage; dimension line (thin, arrowheads ke saath); arrowhead (length : width = 3 : 1, closed & filled); leader line (notes ke liye); dimension figure (value, mm mein, unit nahi likhte).

Types: size dimensions (length, dia) & location dimensions (centres, holes).

Systems: (1) Aligned – values dimension line ke upar, bottom ya right side se padhi jaayein. (2) Unidirectional – sab values horizontal, sirf bottom se padhi jaayein (dimension line beech se tooti).

Arrangement: chain (series), parallel, combined, progressive (from a datum) / co-ordinate.

Exam Tip Rules: har dimension sirf ek baar; hidden lines par dimension mat do; centre line / outline ko dimension line mat banao; dia ke liye Ø, radius ke liye R, square ke liye □; smaller dimension andar, larger bahar (lines cross na karein).

7. Scales

FormulaR.F. = Length on drawingActual length of object (same units) ;   Length of scale = R.F. × Max. length to be measured

Types by RF: full size (1:1), reducing scale (1:2, 1:5, 1:10 …; RF < 1), enlarging scale (2:1, 5:1 …; RF > 1). Scale ka naam drawing par likhna zaroori.

(a) Plain scale – sirf 2 units (e.g. m & dm)

  1. RF aur max length se scale length nikaalo; line draw karo (normally 150 mm ke aas-paas).
  2. Usko main units mein divide karo; zero first division ke end par likho (right side numbering: 1, 2, 3…).
  3. First division ko sub-units mein divide karo (left side numbering).
  4. Scale ki width ~10 mm rakho, name, RF aur units likho; required length mark karke dikhao.
Example RF = 1/60, m & dm padhne hain, max 6 m. Scale length = 600 cm × 1/60 = 10 cm. 10 cm ko 6 equal parts (each = 1 m) mein bato; first part ko 10 parts (each = 1 dm). 3.7 m dikhane ke liye: zero se right 3 main divisions + zero se left 7 sub-divisions.

(b) Diagonal scale – 3 units (e.g. m, dm, cm) – based on similar triangles

Principle: ek short length b ko n equal parts mein divide karna ho to diagonal draw karo; k-th horizontal par diagonal ka horizontal offset = k·b/n. Isse 1/10 of smallest division read hota hai.

  1. Scale length nikaalo, main units mein divide karo, 10 (ya n) parallel horizontal lines equal spacing par draw karo.
  2. First main division ko top & bottom dono par 10 sub-divisions mein bato.
  3. Bottom ke k-th point ko top ke (k+1)-th point se join karo (diagonals).
  4. Reading = main + sub-division + (horizontal line no. × least count).
Example RF = 1/4000, max 500 m, metres tak padhna hai. Scale length = 500 m × 1/4000 = 12.5 cm. 12.5 cm ko 5 parts (each = 100 m) mein bato; first part ko 10 parts (10 m) mein; 10 horizontal divisions se 1 m. 438 m dikhane ke liye: 4 main (400) + 3 sub (30) + 8th horizontal line par diagonal tak.

(c) Scale of chords – angles measure / set karne ke liye (protractor na ho to)

  1. Line AO draw karo; O par perpendicular, radius R se quarter circle (quadrant) A se B tak banao.
  2. Arc AB ko 9 equal parts (each 10°) mein divide karo (pehle 60° & 30° compass se, phir trisect).
  3. A ko centre lekar har division point tak radius (chord) se arc kheecho jo AO extended line ko kaate – ye points 0°, 10°, 20° … 90° ki chord lengths hain.
  4. Note: chord of 60° = radius; angle θ banane ke liye radius = chord of 60° se arc lo aur chord of θ cut karo.

8. Conic Sections

Definition Right circular cone ko different planes se kaatne par mile curves = conics. Eccentricity e = (distance of point from focus) / (distance from directrix). e < 1 → ellipse (plane cuts all generators), e = 1 → parabola (plane ∥ one generator), e > 1 → hyperbola (plane ∥ axis / cuts both nappes).
CurveConstruction methods
Ellipse(1) Concentric circle method (2) Oblong / rectangle method (3) Arcs of circles (foci) method: PF1+PF2 = major axis (4) Eccentricity (directrix-focus) method (5) Parallelogram, loop-of-thread, trammel methods
Parabola(1) Eccentricity (directrix-focus) method (2) Rectangle method (3) Tangent method (4) Parallelogram method
Hyperbola(1) Eccentricity method (2) Rectangular hyperbola (asymptotes at 90°, PV = constant) (3) Foci / arcs method: |PF1 − PF2| = transverse axis

Ellipse by concentric circle method (most asked)

  1. Major axis AB aur minor axis CD ko centre O par perpendicular draw karo; O se dono ke circles (dia = AB & CD) banao.
  2. Dono circles ko 12 equal parts mein radial lines se divide karo.
  3. Bade circle ke point se vertical line, chhote circle ke corresponding point se horizontal line – intersection = ellipse ka point.
  4. Saare points ko French curve se smooth join karo. Foci: C se radius AB/2 se AB par cut → F1, F2.

Eccentricity method (any conic) – steps

  1. Directrix DD aur axis (⊥ DD) draw karo; focus F distance par mark karo. Vertex V ko CF par is tarah divide karo ki VF/VC = e (e.g. e = 2/3 → CF ko 5 parts, V ko C se 3rd division par lo: VC = 3, VF = 2).
  2. V par perpendicular VE = VF lo; CE join karke extend karo (ye line slope e deti hai).
  3. Axis par points 1, 2, 3 … lo; har point par vertical line jo CE extended ko kaate (1′, 2′ …). Distance 1–1′ radius se F centre lekar vertical 1-1′ ko dono side cut karo → curve ke points.
Ellipse – oblong (rectangle) method: rectangle = major × minor axis. Half major axis OA aur rectangle ki side AE (= CD/2) dono ko same no. (e.g. 4) equal parts mein bato, dono ko A se number karo (1, 2, 3). C ko AE ke points se join karo; D (minor axis ka doosra end) se OA ke points se line extend karo; same number wali lines ke intersection = ellipse points (har quarter mein repeat).
Parabola – rectangle method: rectangle (base × axis height) banao. Half base ko n parts aur side ko n parts mein bato (base points axis se, side points vertex wale corner se number karo). Side ke points ko vertex V se join karo; base ke points se axis ke parallel lines – same number ke intersections = parabola points. Hyperbola (rectangular): asymptotes OX, OY; given point P se lines ∥ OX, ∥ OY; O se radial lines – inse PV = const points milte hain.
Tangent & Normal Eccentricity curves: point P ko F se join karo; F par PF ke ⊥ line draw karo jo directrix ko T par mile; TP = tangent, P par TP ke ⊥ = normal. Ellipse (foci method): PF1, PF2 join karo; ∠F1PF2 ka bisector = normal, uske ⊥ = tangent. Parabola: tangent, PF aur P se axis ke parallel line ke beech ke angle ko bisect karta hai.

Cycloid – steps (circle dia D)

  1. Circle draw karo, usse 12 equal parts mein divide karo; base line PA = πD draw karo aur usko bhi 12 parts mein bato.
  2. Centre C se base ke parallel locus of centre line draw karo; uspe C1, C2 … C12 (base divisions ke upar) mark karo.
  3. Circle ke division points se horizontal lines draw karo.
  4. C1 centre, radius D/2 se arc jo 1st horizontal line ko kaate → P1; aise hi C2 se 2nd line par P2 … Smooth curve join karo.

Epi/Hypo: same steps, bas base line ki jagah directing circle ka arc (angle θ) lo, 12 radial divisions, locus of centre ek arc (radius R + r ya R − r) hoga. Involute of circle: circle ko 12 parts mein bato, har point par tangent draw karo, tangent par length = (n/12)·πD mark karo → points join. Archimedean spiral: 12 radial lines aur radius ko 12 equal parts; circle 1 ka radial line 1 se intersection … point join.

Normal & Tangent Cycloid / epi / hypo: point P se radius r leke locus of centre par C cut karo; C se base (ya directing circle radius) par perpendicular → N; PN = normal, ⊥ = tangent. Involute: P se base circle par tangent = normal. Archimedean spiral: constant C = (r1 − r2)/(θ1 − θ2) (θ in radian); O se OP ke ⊥ OM = C lo; MP = normal.

UNIT II Projections, Points, Lines & Planes

1. Types of Projection

TypeProjectorsResult / use

Principal planes: VP (vertical, FV/elevation milta hai), HP (horizontal, TV/plan) aur PP (profile plane, side view). HP & VP ki intersection line = reference line XY. Drawing ke liye HP ko clockwise 90° ghuma kar VP ke saath ek plane mein laate hain.

Missing views: do given views (FV, TV) se teesra view (SV) banana.

  1. Given views mein har corner ko number karo; object ko mentally visualise karo.
  2. FV se horizontal projectors SV ki taraf; TV se horizontal projectors 45° miter line tak, wahan se vertical SV mein (depth transfer).
  3. Intersections join karo; hidden edges dashed, centre lines chain.

3. Projection of Straight Lines

Notation: true length TL (L), true inclination with HP = θ, with VP = φ; FV ka XY se angle = α (apparent), TV ka = β.

Key relations θ + φ ≤ 90° (= 90° → line profile plane mein, FV & TV dono ⊥ XY). Difference of distances of ends from HP = L sin θ; from VP = L sin φ.

True length & true inclinations (line inclined to both) – rotating line method

  1. Given FV a′b′ aur TV ab draw karo (end projectors).
  2. TV ab ko a centre lekar ghumao jab tak ∥ XY na ho (ab1); b1 se vertical projector, b′ ka horizontal locus se mile → b1′. a′b1′ = TL, aur XY se angle = θ.
  3. Isi tarah FV a′b′ ko XY ke ∥ ghumao (a′b2′), project down to b's locus → ab2 = TL aur angle = φ.
  4. Reverse problem (L, θ, φ given): pehle line ko θ par (∥ VP) aur φ par (∥ HP) draw karke lengths L cos θ, L cos φ nikaalo, phir loci ke beech arcs se final views.

Other methods: trapezoid method (TV ke ends par ⊥ heights = FV ki heights → TL) aur auxiliary plane method (X1Y1 ∥ to TV lekar auxiliary FV = TL).

Traces of a line

Definition Line (ya extended line) jahan HP ko mile = Horizontal Trace (HT); jahan VP ko mile = Vertical Trace (VT). Line ∥ HP → HT nahi; ∥ VP → VT nahi; ∥ both → koi trace nahi.
  1. HT: FV a′b′ ko extend karo jab tak XY ko h′ par mile; h′ se vertical projector draw karo jo TV (extended) ko h par mile → h = HT.
  2. VT: TV ab ko extend karo jab tak XY ko v par mile; v se vertical projector FV (extended) ko v′ par mile → v′ = VT.

4. Projection of Planes & Traces

Planes (laminae): triangle, square, rectangle, pentagon, hexagon, circle, semicircle. Types: (i) perpendicular planes – ⊥ to both (profile), ⊥ one & ∥ other, ⊥ one & inclined to other; (ii) oblique plane – inclined to both HP & VP.

Traces of plane: plane (extended) jis line par HP ko kaate = HT, VP ko kaate = VT. Plane ∥ HP → sirf VT (∥ XY); ⊥ HP & inclined VP → HT at φ, VT ⊥ XY; inclined HP & ⊥ VP → VT at θ, HT ⊥ XY. Oblique plane ke HT & VT XY par ek point par milte hain.

Plane positionTrue shape inOther view
∥ HP (⊥ VP)TVFV = line ∥ XY
∥ VP (⊥ HP)FVTV = line ∥ XY
Inclined θ to HP, ⊥ VP– (TV reduced)FV = line at θ

Plane inclined to both HP & VP – change of position (3 stages)

  1. Stage 1: plane ko ∥ HP maan lo (agar θ given hai HP se) – TV true shape, FV line on XY. Given edge/corner ki condition ke hisaab se rakho (e.g. one side ⊥ VP).
  2. Stage 2: FV line ko XY se θ par tilt karo; points project karke naya TV (reduced) banao.
  3. Stage 3: stage-2 ke TV ko φ (ya β) se rotate karo jaisa given hai; FV ke points stage-2 FV ke horizontal loci aur naye TV ke projectors ke intersection se lo.
Common Mistake Edge/diagonal "inclined φ to VP" (true angle) aur "TV makes β with XY" (apparent angle) ko same mat samjho. φ ho to stage 3 mein TL-locus method lagana padta hai.

Auxiliary planes

Auxiliary Inclined Plane (AIP): ⊥ VP, inclined to HP. Auxiliary Vertical Plane (AVP): ⊥ HP, inclined to VP. Inclined surface ka true shape dekhne ke liye naya reference line X1Y1 us surface ke edge-view ke parallel lete hain; projectors X1Y1 ke ⊥ aur distances pichle (dusre) view se X1Y1 tak transfer karte hain → auxiliary view (true shape). Isse plane / line ki TL & true inclination bhi milti hai.

Axis inclined to one plane, parallel to other – steps (e.g. cone, axis 30° to HP, ∥ VP)

  1. Stage 1: solid ko simple position mein lo – axis ⊥ HP, base HP par. TV = circle (12 generators ke points), FV = triangle.
  2. Stage 2: FV ko as it is copy karke is tarah tilt karo ki axis HP se 30° (ya base 60°) par ho; condition dhyan do – "resting on a point of base", "on a generator", "on an edge".
  3. Naye FV ke har point se vertical projectors, aur stage-1 TV ke points se horizontal lines → intersections = naya TV. Visible/hidden edges check karke join karo.

Cylinder (generators ∥ axis, base ellipse dikhega), pyramid/prism (corners/edges number karke) sab mein same procedure. Polyhedra (tetrahedron, cube) bhi isi tarah.

Axis inclined to both HP & VP

  1. Stage 1 & 2 upar jaise (axis θ to HP, ∥ VP).
  2. Stage 3: stage-2 ka TV ko copy karke rotate karo taaki axis ka TV XY se β par ho (agar φ given ho to β pehle TL method se nikaalo). Naye TV ke points se projectors upar, stage-2 FV ke horizontal loci se mila kar final FV.
Yaad Rakho Visibility rule: FV mein jo edges observer ke paas (TV mein XY se door) hain wo visible; TV mein jo edges upar hain (FV mein XY se door) wo visible. Outer boundary hamesha visible hoti hai.

Definition Solid ko imaginary section (cutting) plane se kaat kar andar ka shape dikhana. Kata hua surface = section, uska true shape tab milta hai jab use section plane ke ∥ plane par project karein. Cut surface par 45° thin hatching lines (2–3 mm spacing).

Steps – section plane inclined to HP (e.g. hexagonal pyramid cut by AIP)

  1. Solid ke FV & TV draw karo, sab corners name karo.
  2. FV mein given angle/position par cutting plane (VT) draw karo; jahan ye edges/generators ko kaate wo points 1′, 2′, 3′ … mark karo.
  3. Points ko neeche TV ke corresponding edges par project karo; join karke sectional TV hatch karo (cut part hata kar).
  4. True shape: VT ke ∥ X1Y1 lo; 1′, 2′ … se ⊥ projectors, aur TV mein XY se distances X1Y1 se mark karo → join & hatch.

Cone ke sections: plane through apex → triangle; ∥ base → circle; inclined, all generators cut → ellipse; ∥ to a generator → parabola; ∥ axis (or angle with base > generator angle) → hyperbola. Cylinder: inclined plane → ellipse, ∥ axis → rectangle.

Common Mistake Cone/cylinder par points generators se project karo; agar point FV mein extreme generator par na ho to horizontal circle method (point se ∥ base circle) use karo, warna TV galat aata hai.

4. Intersection (Interpenetration) of Surfaces

Jab ek solid doosre mein ghusta hai to dono surfaces ki common line = curve / line of intersection. Use: pipe joints, ducts, boiler, chimney, machine castings.

(a) Line method: ek solid ki surface par kai lines (edges / generators) lo; har line doosre solid ki surface ko jahan pierce kare wo point dhoondo (us view se jahan doosra solid edge-view / circle mein dikhe). Prism–prism, cylinder–cylinder, prism–cylinder ke liye easy.

(b) Cutting plane method: dono solids ko kai parallel cutting planes se kaato (preferably jo dono par simple sections de – lines ya circles). Har plane par dono sections ke intersection points = curve ke points. Cone–cylinder, cone–prism ke liye best (horizontal planes → cone par circle).

CaseResult / tip
Prism – prismIntersection straight line segments; edge points line method se, phir visibility se join
Cylinder – cylinder (axes ⊥, intersecting)Curve; equal diameters → FV mein straight lines (45° V shape)
Cylinder – prismPrism edges cylinder ko kahan kaatte hain (TV circle se), phir intermediate lines
Cone – cylinder / cone – prismCutting plane method (horizontal planes): cone section = circle, cylinder section = rectangle lines
Exam Tip Pehle "critical points" (extreme top/bottom/front/back aur outlines par) zaroor locate karo; curve unhi se visible/hidden part mein divide hota hai.

UNIT IV Development of Surfaces & Isometric Projection

1. Development of Surfaces

Definition Solid ki saari surfaces ko ek plane par khol kar (unfold) true size mein dikhana = development. Sheet-metal work (buckets, funnels, ducts, hoppers, chimneys) mein pattern banane ke liye. Development mein har line true length mein hoti hai. Sirf developable surfaces (prism, cylinder, pyramid, cone) exactly khulte hain; sphere approximate.
MethodUsed forIdea
Parallel linePrisms, cylindersLateral edges/generators parallel; stretch-out line = perimeter, verticals par heights transfer
Radial linePyramids, conesEdges/generators apex se radiate; slant edge/height ko radius lekar arc
TriangulationTransition pieces, oblique conesSurface ko chhote triangles mein todo, har triangle ka TL se true shape bana kar sequence mein lagao
ApproximateSphere (zone / lune method)Non-developable; segments approximate
Formula Cylinder: rectangle πD × H. Cone (base radius r, slant height L = √(r² + h²)): sector of radius L, θ = 360° × r / L. Cube: 6 equal squares (cross / T shape). Prism: rectangles side by side, total length = perimeter of base. Pyramid: n triangles with radius = true slant edge length.

Steps – development of lateral surface of a cut (truncated) cone

  1. FV & TV draw karo; TV circle ko 12 parts mein bato, generators o′1′, o′2′ … FV mein draw karo.
  2. Radius = L (slant height) aur angle θ = 360 r/L se sector banao; sector ko 12 parts mein bato, generators O–1, O–2 … draw karo.
  3. Cutting plane jahan generators ko kaate (FV mein), un points ko true length par laane ke liye horizontal line extreme generator (o′a′, jo TL hai) tak le jao; O se wo TL distance development ke corresponding generator par mark karo.
  4. Points ko smooth curve se join karo; jo part hataya gaya use thin/dashed dikhao. Prism/cylinder ke parts mein heights seedhe verticals par transfer hoti hain.

Pyramid development: slant edge ka TL nikaalo (edge ko FV mein extreme edge par ghuma kar); radius = TL se arc, uspar base side length n baar cut karo, apex se join. Cut pyramid mein cut points ko true length ke liye extreme (TL) edge par horizontal shift karo.

Transition pieces

Do alag shapes ya sizes ke openings ko jodne wala piece – e.g. square-to-round, round-to-rectangle (ducts, hoppers, chimney). Development triangulation se: round end ko 12 parts mein, har corner of square ko nearest circle points se join karke triangles + flat triangles bante hain; har line ka TL (TL diagram: horizontal = TV length, vertical = height) nikaal kar triangles ek-ek karke lagao.

2. Isometric Projection

Definition Pictorial orthographic projection jisme cube ka body diagonal VP ke ⊥ hota hai, isliye teeno mutually perpendicular edges VP se equally inclined hote hain aur equally foreshortened. FV mein ek hi view mein teeno faces dikhti hain.
  • Isometric axes: teen lines aapas mein 120° par – ek vertical, do horizontal se 30° par.
  • Isometric lines: iso axes ke parallel lines (inpe hi measurement hoti hai). Non-isometric lines: parallel nahi (inclined edges) – unke end points coordinates se locate karke join karo (box / offset method).
  • Isometric planes: do iso axes se bane plane – top (horizontal), left & right vertical faces.
Isometric scaleIso length = cos 45°cos 30° × TL = √(2/3) × TL ≈ 0.816 TL

Construction: horizontal line se 45° aur 30° lines draw karo; 45° line par true divisions (mm/cm) mark karo; har point se vertical drop karo 30° line tak → iso divisions.

Isometric VIEW (drawing)Isometric PROJECTION
Iso lines par true lengths (ordinary scale)Iso lines par isometric lengths (0.816 × true, iso scale)
Bada dikhta hai (≈ 22.5% larger)Actual projection – exact size
Sphere: circle of radius ≈ 1.22 RSphere: circle of true radius R

Isometric of plane figures & solids

  • Rectangle/square/polygon: enclosing rectangle (box) iso axes par banao, corners offsets se locate karo. Hexagon/pentagon ke liye box method.
  • Circle → ellipse by four-centre method: circle ke around square ka isometric (rhombus) banao; obtuse-angle corners se opposite sides ke mid-points tak lines → do centres yahi corners, aur in lines ke intersection do aur centres; char arcs se ellipse. Ya offset / co-ordinate method (points plotting).
  • Cylinder: top & bottom ellipses (four-centre), common tangents (extreme lines) se join; hidden half bottom ellipse dashed/omit.
  • Cone: base ellipse + apex (axis par height mark) se ellipse ko tangent lines.
  • Pyramid: base iso mein, centre se axis ∥ vertical iso axis par height, apex ko base corners se join (slant edges non-isometric).
  • Prism: base iso mein draw karke har corner se axis ki direction mein length ∥ lines, top face join.
  • Sphere: isometric projection = circle of radius R. Combination (e.g. sphere on cube/frustum): sphere ka contact point aur centre iso scale se locate karo (centre contact point se iso R upar), phir true R se circle.
  • Combination of solids: neeche wale solid se shuru karo, upar wale ka base centre axis par locate karke draw karo; hidden lines generally nahi dikhate.
Common Mistake Non-isometric line (jaise pyramid ka slant edge, ya inclined face) par direct length mat napo – sirf iso lines par measure hota hai. Isometric drawing mein angle bhi true nahi dikhte.

UNIT V Computer Aided Drawing & Computer Graphics

1. CAD, CAED & CAE

TermMeaning
CAD – Computer Aided Design / DraftingComputer ka use design create, modify, analyse aur optimise karne ke liye; 2D drafting + 3D modelling (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA, Creo).
CAED – Computer Aided Engineering Design (/ Drafting)CAD ka engineering design wala part: geometric modelling + engineering calculations + automated drafting, design ko documentation tak le jana.
CAE – Computer Aided EngineeringDesign ka computer par analysis/simulation – FEA (stress), CFD (flow), kinematics, thermal (ANSYS, Abaqus) – prototype banane se pehle.

Design process mein CAD ka role

Design cycle: recognition of need → definition → synthesis → analysis & optimisation → evaluation → presentation. CAD mein: geometric modelling (synthesis), engineering analysis (CAE), design review & evaluation (interference check, animation), automated drafting (drawings, BOM).

2. Elements & Essential Requirements of CAD

Elements (components) of a CAD system:

  • Hardware – CPU/workstation, memory, graphics card, input devices, display, output (printer/plotter), storage.
  • Software – OS, graphics package (2D/3D), application & analysis software, database.
  • User / designer – jo system ko operate kare (human creativity).
  • Network & data-exchange (IGES, STEP, DXF).

Essential requirements: fast processor & sufficient RAM, high-resolution graphics display, accurate input devices, large storage, reliable software with standard drafting features (layers, snaps, dimensioning, libraries), good user interface, data security/back-up, trained operator, hard-copy output devices.

Integrated CAD / CAM

CAM = Computer Aided Manufacturing (CNC part programming, process planning, robots, inspection). Integrated CAD/CAM mein ek hi common database design se manufacturing tak use hota hai – CAD model se seedha CNC tool-path (G-code) bante hain. Pura plant (design, planning, production, QC, business) jud jaye to CIM (Computer Integrated Manufacturing).

Necessity, importance & applications

Necessity / importance (advantages): productivity badhti hai, drafting time kam; high accuracy; easy modification & reuse (copy, mirror, array); better visualisation (3D, shading, animation); standardisation & libraries; analysis possible; easy storage, retrieval, sharing; less paper; lower cost & time-to-market; CAM se direct link.

Applications: mechanical (machine parts, assembly, CNC), civil & architecture (building plans, structures, GIS), electrical/electronics (PCB, circuits, VLSI), automobile & aerospace (body design, simulation), textile & fashion, ship building, plant layout & piping, animation & medical imaging.

Yaad Rakho CAD systems ke types: mainframe-based, minicomputer-based, workstation-based aur PC-based. Modelling ke types: 2D drafting, 3D wireframe (sirf edges), surface model (skin) aur solid model (mass, volume, interference check possible – sabse complete).

4. Display Devices

Cathode Ray Tube (CRT)

Parts: electron gun (heated cathode + control grid), focusing system, deflection system (electrostatic/magnetic plates – horizontal & vertical), phosphor-coated screen. Electron beam phosphor ko hit kare to light emit hoti hai; light jaldi decay hoti hai isliye picture ko baar-baar refresh karna padta hai (refresh CRT). Persistence = light ke 1/10 tak girne ka time; resolution = max points/pixels display; aspect ratio = vertical : horizontal points.

Random scan (vector / calligraphic)Raster scan
Beam sirf un points par jaata hai jahan line draw karni haiBeam pure screen ko row-by-row (left→right, top→bottom) sweep karta hai
Picture = line-drawing commands in display file / refresh bufferPicture = intensity values of all pixels in frame buffer (bit-map)
Refresh 30–60 times/s; smooth, high-resolution linesRefresh 60–80 frames/s (interlaced possible); lines staircase (jaggies/aliasing)
Realistic shaded/filled images mushkil; costlyShading, colour, filled areas easy; cheap; TV technology
Use: line drawings, CAD wireframes (old)Use: almost all modern monitors

CRT monitors (colour): (1) Beam penetration – red & green phosphor layers, beam speed se colour (only 4 colours, random scan). (2) Shadow mask – 3 electron guns (R, G, B), phosphor dots triad, shadow mask ke holes se har beam apne colour dot par – millions of colours (raster displays).

DVST (Direct View Storage Tube): picture storage grid par charge distribution ke roop mein store; primary (writing) gun pattern banata hai, flood gun continuously screen ko illuminate rakhta hai → refresh ki zaroorat nahi, flicker-free, complex drawings high resolution par. Drawbacks: selective erase nahi (poora screen erase karna padta hai), colour nahi, low contrast, animation possible nahi.

Flat panel displays: CRT se kam volume, weight & power. Emissive (electrical energy → light): plasma panel (gas-discharge, neon cells), thin-film electroluminescent, LED. Non-emissive: LCD – liquid crystal light ko polarise karke pass/block karta hai (backlight chahiye); passive & active-matrix (TFT). Laptops, tablets, modern monitors.
